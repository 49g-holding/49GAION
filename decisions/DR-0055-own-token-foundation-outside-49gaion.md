## Decision

Entry 11-03-k (the governing seat, 2026-11-03, Founder Mind room): OWN Token Foundation and the whole OWN token operation are outside the organisation (`[ORG]`) and outside 49GAION: independent and self-controlled. It is not an AIOC, not a Cell, and not a subsidiary of the organisation. It runs as its own group, 24/7 and automatically; only the mint (and mainnet contract deploys/changes), payments, total supply, shares, the four mint addresses and the vesting schedule need the HOAC FOUNDER's approval.

1. **Out of scope.** No part, AIOC, Cell or register of 49GAION holds the OWN Token Foundation or the OWN token operation. Where any earlier working text listed a Token Foundation as an AIOC, that listing is removed by this record.
2. **What stays with the HOAC FOUNDER.** The mint, mainnet contract deploys and changes, payments, total supply, shares, the four mint addresses and the vesting schedule.
