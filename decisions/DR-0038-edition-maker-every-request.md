## Decision

1. **Every request starts at Edition Maker.** Every order, every request made to 49GAION, is processed by 49GAION Edition Maker, whether it comes from one person with one task, a team, a company or a group of companies. Edition Maker belongs to 49GAION itself: what an organisation receives is not Edition Maker, but its own `[ORG]AION`.
2. **Its intake.** Edition Maker takes in what the organisation is for through its own intake stages, set in its requirements.
3. **The core it designs and gives back.** From that intake it designs the first core of the organisation's `[ORG]AION`: a first AIOC at the level of the governing seat (GOV), delivered as one AI workspace, with its capabilities written in it. The core holds:
   - the first version of the organisation's own Standard and of its Blueprint;
   - a direct link to the central repository of 49GAION, its Standard and its Library;
   - the mindset of 49GAION, in which it is trained.

   From there on, the core is trained and ready to build its organisation and keep it under control, by its own standards and principles. It proposes, and a human seat decides (HF-01).
4. **Its work, in three stages.**
   1. It builds the organisation.
   2. It follows the organisation until it becomes a successful organisation.
   3. In the end, it is the central hub of reporting and oversight of that organisation.

   Through all three it plans the operation and never runs it (DR-0035).
5. **What stays.** Everything else in the master definition, section 2.5, stays as it was:
   - every case becomes an AION;
   - the main work of laying the foundations of the Standard and of the AION way of seeing;
   - no finished design or configuration;
   - the way of seeing;
   - the handover;
   - the Library as a marketplace;
   - what an organisation comes for.
6. **Where it is written.**
   - `49GAION.md`: section 1, the list of parts; section 2.5, items 1 and 2.
   - The Standard: §1.2, row 6; §1.3, item 1; the glossary row "Edition Maker".
   - The Protocol: the row of Edition Maker in the Library register.
7. **Version.** Unreleased `v1.8.0`. The version labels and `state.json` stay at `v1.7.0` until GOV releases.
