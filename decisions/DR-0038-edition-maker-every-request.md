## Decision

1. **Every request for an `[ORG]AION` starts at Edition Maker.** Edition Maker is 49GAION's own asset, and it is given to no one. Every request goes into it, whether it comes from one person with one task, a team, a company or a group of companies.
2. **What it gives back.** The organisation's `[ORG]AION`: a first AIOC at the level of the governing seat (GOV), delivered as one AI workspace, with its capabilities written in it. The mindset of 49GAION is instilled in it, and it is linked directly to the standard Blueprint and the central Library of 49GAION. From there on, it is ready to build its organisation and keep it under control, by its own standards and principles. It proposes, and a human seat decides (HF-01).
3. **Its work, in three stages.**
   1. It builds the organisation.
   2. It follows the organisation until it becomes a successful organisation.
   3. In the end, it is the central hub of reporting and oversight of that organisation.

   Through all three it plans the operation and never runs it (DR-0035).
