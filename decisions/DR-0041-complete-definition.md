- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)

  - "We never introduce a one-person organisation; we define everything by the legal positions in the organisation. The owner has, in the end, the right to say to whom the legal positions are assigned; he may assign all of them to one person."
  - "The place of the Blueprints, the organisation's own Standard and its Library stays at the governance seat, inside its panel." Later: "Each organisation's Blueprint, Standard and Library sit at the governance level, where its AION sits, and the AIOCs relate to them there."

  - "Every change in these is made with the governance seat's approval, but Top Management, and indeed every member, may propose, because it comes from a living system, from inside."   - "The first AIOC builds everything, or plans it, or puts its tools in place, because it is the central hub of the operation, the human hub and the AI hub."

## Decision

1. **The complete definition is approved.** It is `49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md`, in this repository. Where any other text of 49GAION differs from it, the complete definition governs, and that text is read with it until it is brought into line.
2. **The policy.** 49GAION writes principles and the few safeguards that always hold, not procedures. The details and the way of working are decided by AI at the time, and changed by it as AI improves.
3. **What it settles.**
   1. **Positions, never a "one-person organisation".** The right to fill positions comes from the owner, through the governance seat, which appoints Top Management directly and keeps the final right over any position. One person may hold several positions, or all of them, where the law allows; the positions stay distinct; independence, where needed, is real and held from outside where no holder inside can be.
   2. **The organisation's rules are its IP.** Its Standard, Blueprint and Library sit at its governance level, where its `[ORG]AION` sits; the AIOCs relate to them there. Proposing is open to all; the `[ORG]AION` rejects with a reason, or applies with the governance seat's approval; the seat's dial is its signed standing approval for small changes. Top Management sends its achievements to be recorded there.
   3. **The core is the root.** The first AIOC, at the governance level, made from the Library part 49GAION Charter, with the gateway and the stop. It holds the human position of the governance seat, which its AI serves and never holds.
   4. **The Top Management Group is the hub of the operation,** human and AI: the first AIOC under the core, which builds everything in the operation. 49GAION requires no separate AI hub unit; where one exists (`[ORG]AIOC-OCO`), it serves the operation under Top Management.
   5. **Panels and back offices.** The panel is the organisation's own code interface; the AI chat workspace is an authorised interface and the back office that keeps the panel; each covers the other.
   6. **Capability 2, Governance and Delegation:** one governance; the dial; the mandate and its envelope; one counterparty; what governance keeps; the gate; the governance veto.
   7. **The `[ORG]AION` is not bare.** The Library is its greatest asset; its first work is the first proposal, a small organisation designed from 49GAION's patterns and the governance seat's brief. The handover is complete when the seat accepts the edition.
   8. **"Powered by 49GAION"** follows any use of 49GAION.
4. **Nothing is removed.** The other texts of 49GAION are brought into line by labelled additions and reading notes; superseded wording stays, with its label.
5. **The test.** The cold-read test (`references/2026-10-25-test-cold-read.md`) is the acceptance of the complete definition, and of the repositories once they are in line.
6. **Version.** Unreleased. The version labels and `state.json` stay at `v1.7.0` until GOV releases.
