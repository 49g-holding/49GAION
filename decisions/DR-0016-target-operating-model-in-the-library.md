## Decision

1. **The part.** **49GAION Target Operating Model** is a Library part, with its card in `49GAION-protocol` (`library/cards/target-operating-model.part-card.json`, `DRAFT`, `last_result` `NOT_RUN`) and its rules in the Protocol section of the same name. In an `[ORG]AION` it is the AIOC `[ORG]AIOC-TOM`.
2. **The second stage.** It is the stage after Source Lifecycle. It designs the target operating model of an organisation of any shape and names roles and what each decides, with a role register that the next stage reads first. It does not say what fills a role.
3. **Sources.** It takes only sources served by reference from Source Lifecycle, and asks what is missing through it. Nothing typed or spoken to it is a source.
4. **Linked directly to Process and Position Design.** On filing a decided release into Source Lifecycle, it also sends a release notice to Process and Position Design, by reference (ID, version and hash). It receives that part's returns directly, under the Library's design return contract (DR-0018), and shows them with their ages.
5. **The return loop ends.** A return is never left open. It goes back and forth in rounds until it ends in one of: answered by a new decided release; answered by a decision of the Requesting Authority; or set aside by that decision and carried forward on a stated basis (a known defect or a decided assumption), so that the stage after it delivers. The loop has its rounds, deadlines and escalation to the Requesting Authority.
6. **Decided and filed.** Every element is decided by the Requesting Authority. The release is frozen, content-addressed and filed into Source Lifecycle as a source.
7. **Where it sits.** The Standard carries the part in §3.1, item 4, and in the terms table; the Protocol in the section of the same name, with the card and its fixed test, TEST-TARGET-OPERATING-MODEL v1. Its mother blueprint is in its own writing repository; its next version follows this record.
8. **Version.** The texts sit on main under the unreleased `v1.6.0`. The version labels and `state.json` stay at `v1.5.0` until GOV releases.
