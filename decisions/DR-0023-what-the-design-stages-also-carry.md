# DR-0023 · 49GAION — What the design stages also carry: strategy, delivery, priority, value, and the work as it runs

- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the directions of the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION of 2026-10-15 (translated): "the target operating model must also take, as an input, the capability of observing the organisation that runs now; if there is none, it stays empty"; "what can be given to Process and Position Design, give to it; what can be given to the target operating model, give to it; what remains, we must build"; and "where a capability's real home is in our earlier parts, add it there too", on a study of fifteen comparable products whose features were placed stage by stage. And the direction of 2026-10-16 to bring the three 49GAION repositories up to date with the design-stage work (DR-0019). On the proposal drafted in the writing repository of the Target Operating Model (its `upstream/PROPOSALS.md`, item 10), made there in its mother blueprint, version 0.6.0, and in the mother blueprints of Process and Position Design (version 0.5.0) and Delivery and Implementation (version 0.1.2), and in Source Lifecycle's first instance.
- **Relation:** builds on DR-0014, DR-0016 to DR-0019; it supersedes nothing.
- **Number:** this record takes the next free number at merge, after DR-0022

## Decision

1. **What the target operating model also states** (Standard §3.1, item 4). The organisation's strategy on one page (purpose, values, long-range target, three-year picture, one-year goals with their measures, critical numbers); how each capability is delivered (in-house, shared service, outsourced, partner) and the make-or-buy that follows, never naming a provider; how mature each capability is now and should be; which value streams are critical, each for a stated reason; each outside party's journey through the organisation, from crossings already declared; a value case per outcome; and a priority per element (must, should, could), proposed by a fixed rule and decided with the element. Each comes from the organisation's sources or decisions; the part never writes a goal, a target, a level or a figure of its own, and what the sources lack is shown as not given, unknown or not set.
2. **The work as it runs.** The target operating model may read records of the work as it runs today, exported by role-type and process, never about a person. Source Lifecycle records such an export only as the organisation's own projection, which removes who did the work, after an impact assessment and the decision of the position that holds data protection (HF-17); an export that still says who did the work is refused. Where there is none, the part says so and goes on. No part watches work.
3. **Process and Position Design reads them** (Standard §3.1, item 3). Its objectives start from the strategy; the critical value streams are designed first, in order of priority; a capability delivered outside becomes a hand-off with oversight steps, never an in-house process; today's records of work are read beside the designed workload, never about a person.
4. **Delivery and Implementation carries them** (Standard §3.1, item 5). The value case and the priority are carried unchanged; its register of value starts from the value case, and its rollout follows the priority.
5. **Source Lifecycle also keeps** (Standard §3.1, item 7). The organisation's dictionary, its own terms with their definitions and sources, served by reference to every stage; and an essentials checklist (strategy, plan, measures, structure of positions, processes, meetings, dictionary) that flags what an organisation's sources should hold, never filling it.
6. **Left open, never filled** (Standard §3.1, item 10). What one design stage leaves open (not given, not decided, not set, unknown, no observation), no later stage fills.
7. **Version.** Unreleased `v1.6.0`, as DR-0019. The Protocol texts (§13, §17, §19, §20, §21), the cards and their fixed tests follow in `49GAION-protocol` after this merges.
