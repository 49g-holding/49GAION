# DR-0025 · 49GAION — Built from the smallest units; the Library's Parts and Products

- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the directions of the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION of 2026-10-16 (translated): "The philosophy of 49GAION is that it builds the smallest units and keeps their technology; then it can combine and merge those small units, in different models, to make an output. These four stages we built, put together, make a product that can be named and defined in our Standard, with features we know; its blueprint says: these, these, these and these, placed beside each other, become this." "When we go to the next product, for example for the AION itself, we may again use all of these AIOCs, or some of them, in it." Source Lifecycle alone is a product; the four together are a product that gets a name; we build it later." "For the Library, use whatever is standard." ## Decision

1. **How 49GAION builds.** 49GAION builds the smallest units (Cells, Pods, AIOCs) and keeps each with its own technology: its blueprint, its card, its fixed test, its repository and its version. A unit is built once and kept. Outputs are made by combining units, in different arrangements, never by rebuilding them.
2. **The Library in two sections.** The 49GAION Library is the one store of everything 49GAION has built and knows. It has two sections:
   - **Parts.** Everything 49GAION builds, from a Cell to an AIOC, with its blueprint, card, fixed test, contracts and method catalogues, and its whole history: versions, lineage, decisions, lessons and test results. It is the toolbox and the knowledge of 49GAION.
   - **Products.** What the Library offers. A product is a single part offered alone (Source Lifecycle, which runs on its own, is one), or an **assembly**: parts placed side by side and connected.
3. **An assembly.** Its blueprint lists its parts, each by pin (repository, path, version, commit, SHA-256), the connections between them (which contract joins which two parts), what the assembly itself sets, and one shared test across its parts. Its features are its parts' features. Each part still works alone. A new version of a part moves only its pin, and the shared test runs again. A part may serve in more than one product. A product is named and defined before it is built; once built, the Library holds it, and it may itself be a part of a later product.
4. **The first assembly: 49GAION Organisation Design and Delivery.** Four parts placed side by side: Source Lifecycle, Target Operating Model, Process and Position Design, and Delivery and Implementation. They are connected by the release notice and design return contracts, as the Protocol section The design chain (§21) sets, and TEST-DESIGN-CHAIN is their shared test. Its features, from its parts:
   - an organisation's own sources (its documents, and its people's words once they approve them) recorded, referenced and fixed, each with one valid version;
   - a decided target operating model built from those sources: capabilities, value streams, entities and their crossings, the strategy on one page, delivery models, maturity, outside parties' journeys, a value case and a priority per element;
   - processes, then the positions that carry them, designed from that decided model and grouped into units;
   - the decided design taken into a running organisation: gaps, procedures, briefs for counsel and finance, a transition plan, go-live criteria, and answers grounded in the design;
   - throughout: positions only; AI prepares and human positions decide; nothing invented, and what the sources lack stays open; each stage runs alone; every defect returns to the stage that made it.
5. **For human organisations.** Its output is an organisation of human positions, designed and delivered with AI tools. It is not yet an AI-native operation: it does not decide which positions an AI fills. The next product, for an `[ORG]AION`, may use all or some of its parts.
6. **Named now, built later.** The assembly is named and defined now and built from its parts later. Its parts are `DRAFT`, `NOT_RUN`; Delivery and Implementation is written to slice 1 of 4. This records what is built; it is not yet a rule set.
7. **Reading of earlier texts.** "The Library holds all ready-made AIOC blueprints and part cards" (Standard §1.2; §3.1, item 1) reads: those are its Parts, beside its Products. "Library part" everywhere means an item of the Parts section.
8. **What changes.** Standard §1.2 (addition after the table); §1.7, item 16 (new); §3.1, item 1 (addition), items 11 and 12 (new); the terms rows "Parts", "Products", "Assembly" and "49GAION Organisation Design and Delivery"; the index row. After this merges, the Library register in `49GAION-protocol` is set out in the two sections.
9. **Version.** Unreleased `v1.6.0`, as DR-0019.
