# DR-0025 · 49GAION — Built from the smallest units; the Library's parts and products

- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the direction of the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION of 2026-10-16 (translated): "The philosophy of 49GAION is that it builds the smallest units and keeps their technology; then it can combine and merge those small units, in different models, to make an output. These four stages we built, put together, make a product that can be named and defined in our Standard, with features we know; its blueprint says: these, these, these and these, placed beside each other, become this." And: "When we go to the next product, for example for the AION itself, we may again use all of these AIOCs, or some of them, in it." And: "Our Library should have two parts: one for everything we build, and another output, the Library's products. Source Lifecycle alone is a product; the four together are a product that gets a name; we build it later." And, the same day: "At this stage we are not setting hard rules; we are writing what we have achieved." The four stages "were built for human companies, with AI tools; they are not yet an AI-native operation."

## Decision

1. **How 49GAION builds.** 49GAION builds the smallest units (Cells, Pods, AIOCs, Library parts) and keeps each with its own technology: its blueprint, its card, its fixed test, its repository and its version. A unit is built once and kept. Outputs are made by combining units, in different arrangements, never by rebuilding them.
2. **A composed product.** A composed product is a named product made by placing units side by side and connecting them. Its blueprint lists its units, each by pin (repository, path, version, commit, SHA-256), the connections between them (which contract joins which two units), what the composed product itself sets, and one shared test across its units. Its features are its units' features. Each unit still works alone. A new version of a unit moves only its pin, and the shared test runs again. A composed product may itself be a unit of a larger one. A unit may serve in more than one composed product: the next product, for an `[ORG]AION`, may use all or some of the same units.
3. **The first composed product: 49GAION Organisation Design and Delivery.** Four Library parts placed side by side: Source Lifecycle, Target Operating Model, Process and Position Design, and Delivery and Implementation. They are connected by the release notice and design return contracts, as the Protocol section The design chain (§21) sets, and TEST-DESIGN-CHAIN is their shared test. Its features, from its units:
   - an organisation's own sources (its documents, and its people's words once they approve them) recorded, referenced and fixed, each with one valid version;
   - a decided target operating model built from those sources: capabilities, value streams, entities and their crossings, the strategy on one page, delivery models, maturity, outside parties' journeys, a value case and a priority per element;
   - processes, then the positions that carry them, designed from that decided model and grouped into units;
   - the decided design taken into a running organisation: gaps, procedures, briefs for counsel and finance, a transition plan, go-live criteria, and answers grounded in the design;
   - throughout: positions only; AI prepares and human positions decide; nothing invented, and what the sources lack stays open; each stage runs alone; every defect returns to the stage that made it.
4. **Two parts of the Library.** The Library holds two parts:
   - **the parts**: everything 49GAION builds and keeps: units, mother blueprints, cards, contracts and method catalogues;
   - **the products**: what the Library offers. A product is one part offered alone (Source Lifecycle, which runs on its own, is one) or a composed product. A product is named and defined before it is built; it is then built from its parts, and becomes a held object of the Library like any part.
5. **For human organisations.** Its output is an organisation of human positions, designed and delivered with AI tools. It is not yet an AI-native operation: it does not decide which positions an AI fills. An `[ORG]AION` may later compose it with the AI-onizing of positions (each position AI, human or both), the building of AIOCs and their assembly.
6. **Not yet a rule set; built later.** This records what is built. The composed product is named and defined now and built from its parts later. Its units are `DRAFT`, `NOT_RUN`; Delivery and Implementation is written to slice 1 of 4. The Protocol and Library forms of a composed product follow when they are needed.
7. **What changes.** Standard §1.7, item 16 (new); §3.1, items 11 and 12 (new); the terms rows "Composed product", "49GAION Organisation Design and Delivery" and "AI-onize"; the index row.
8. **Version.** Unreleased `v1.6.0`, as DR-0019.
