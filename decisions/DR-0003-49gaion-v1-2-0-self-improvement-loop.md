# DR-0003 · 49GAION v1.2.0 — the improvement loop of Self-Improvement

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in `registers/HOAC-REGISTER.md`)
- **Source:** board discussion, 2026-10-12, about 21:15 Dubai time

## Decision

1. **The improvement loop.** Self-Improvement (Standard §2) gains a working method, Standard §2.3. Every process that can be improved is written in four parts: role, instruction, contract and order. The version in force is the champion. A change is written as a challenger, contracts and order first; it runs in shadow beside the champion, is judged in pairs with reasons, and is revised on the whole history of judgments for a bounded number of rounds.
2. **The golden set and the assurance unit.** Every process has a golden set of real past cases, frozen and held out. A challenger is judged on it, blind, by an assurance unit independent of the proposer and the judge, on a different model provider where one is available, with sampled human review. The assurance unit's verdicts never enter the rounds they measure. No unit grades its own work; `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` never grades the AIOCs it builds.
3. **Promotion stays human.** A challenger reaches the human seat that approves the change (Standard §2.2) only when it wins on the golden set by a set margin, passes every fixed test and every Human Floor test, and costs no more per case unless that seat accepts the cost. On approval it becomes the champion, as a new version; the former champion is kept for rollback. After a promotion there is a dwell time; any accountable human seat may order a rollback at once.
4. **Distillation.** At set intervals, what the best runs did is written into the process's defaults; every failure seen becomes a fixed test; generalised lessons go to the 49GAION Library.
5. **Safeguards.** Standard §2.4 sets the safeguards: against shared blind spots, noisy search, judge drift and tuning to the test. The loop never widens AI authority by itself; no AI approves a promotion, changes a fixed test or grants itself access.
6. **Basis.** The loop follows published research on recursive self-improvement through multi-agent self-supervision. The sources, what was verified, and what was and was not taken are in `references/2026-10-12-references-self-improvement.md`. Fine-tuning model weights on an organisation's own runs is not part of the Standard.
7. **Version.** The 49GAION Standard is `v1.2.0`. The Operating Protocol and the AIOC definition and blueprint template are unchanged and stay `v1.1.0`.
