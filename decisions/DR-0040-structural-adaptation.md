# DR-0040 · 49GAION — `[ORG]AION` capability: Structural Adaptation

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the directions of the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION of 2026-10-25 (translated):
  - "The biggest problem of organisations today is that they lack balance as they grow: either they focus hard on product and users and reach a point where they cannot operate it, or, the other way round, they invest in operation too early, when it is not needed, and lose the other parts."
  - "The first definition of our `[ORG]AION` is that it always keeps the system in balance with its resources and its needs, and grows it over time. On day one it does not impose divisions, departments and costs that are not yet needed."
  - "In that period it arranges the system in smaller teams, faster and more agile, while it watches all the time; as a standard, a system or a process grows, it grows it."
  - "It does this with independent AIOCs. At the early levels it may put several processes in one AIOC; with growth it starts to take processes out of an AIOC and make an independent AIOC."
  - "This is the first evolutionary thing that the `[ORG]AION`, at the governance level, keeps and handles through the life of that organisation."
  - "Give this capability a name, and define it as one of the capabilities of the `[ORG]AION`. Complete it yourself and with your reviewers." On the name: "Take the name from them."
- **Relation:** it adds to DR-0035 (the living core) and DR-0038 (the three stages of its work): it is how the core builds the organisation and follows it until it becomes a successful organisation. It changes nothing else.
- **Number:** this record takes the next free number at merge, after DR-0039

## Decision

1. **The capabilities of an `[ORG]AION` are named.** Each is written in the Standard, §1.3, item 1, and summarised in `49GAION.md`, under "What an `[ORG]AION` can do".
2. **The first capability is Structural Adaptation.** The `[ORG]AION` proposes, at every stage of the organisation's life, the structure that keeps it in balance with its resources and its needs. The structure grows as the work grows and shrinks as the work shrinks, by split and merge of AIOCs. Its rules:
   - start small;
   - watch and measure, today's load and the demand already signed or forecast;
   - act on thresholds the organisation sets, held for a set period, with time to settle after a change;
   - weigh no change, repair, more capacity, a split and a merge, each with its gain and its cost;
   - split when a process outgrows its home;
   - merge with care, never a part with the part that checks it;
   - show the balance and do not run it;
   - every split and merge is decided and signed by a human seat.

   The full text is in the Standard, §1.3, item 1.
3. **The name.** It was written by one engine and challenged by two others (OpenAI, Gemini). The name was taken from the challengers, as GOV directed. "Structural Adaptation" was chosen over "Structural Scaling" because the capability is evolutionary, and it covers shrinking and deliberate non-change as well as growth. The challenges are in `references/2026-10-25-reviews-structural-adaptation.md`.
4. **Where it is written.**
   - The Standard: §1.3, item 1, and the glossary row "Structural Adaptation".
   - `49GAION.md`: a new section 2.6, "What an `[ORG]AION` can do".
   - The Protocol: the Edition Maker PRD, so that the core's instruction carries it.
5. **Version.** Unreleased `v1.8.0`. The version labels and `state.json` stay at `v1.7.0` until GOV releases.
