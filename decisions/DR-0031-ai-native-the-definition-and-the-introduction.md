## Decision

1. **What an organisation is.** An organisation is what reaches and holds the capability to produce a product or a service for someone outside itself: repeatable, of high quality, and not dependent on any one person.
   - **The final output counts.** What counts is the final output that arrives.
   - **Quality.** Quality is a stated standard, set by the recipient's needs. It includes the principles the work must respect (law, safety and fairness), and those principles are never traded away.
   - **The second product.** The capability to deliver the output again is the organisation's second product.
   - **Keeping it.** A successful organisation keeps that capability alive. It has an accountable seat, it measures itself and improves, it adapts as its world changes, and it sustains itself.
2. **Human-native and AI-native.** The difference is where the design starts.
   - **A human-native organisation** starts from people and the jobs they do, and adds AI to them as a tool.
   - **An AI-native organisation** starts from the final output and from what AI can do, and adds people where they are needed. It is designed from the start so that AI produces its output. Inside the work, humans increase the power of AI and cover its gaps. Above the work, humans own the organisation, set its output, its quality and its limits, and answer for it.
3. **Responsible and accountable.** "AI is responsible for the output" means that AI produces it. A human seat is accountable for every output, and no AI accepts accountability (HF-01, unchanged).
4. **What AI-native is not.** It does not mean removing the human. It does not mean that AI is always better. It is the right use of AI, and an organisation that already runs can be redesigned this way, output by output.
5. **How 49GAION builds an organisation.**
   1. It starts from the final output and its quality. Humans set the output, its quality and the limits, a standard list that includes the Human Floor.
   2. It builds the organisation first on what AI can do. The design works back from the final output and corrects each step before it. The processes are built from the capabilities of AI, not from the experience of human organisations.
   3. It covers the gaps of AI with human seats marked HOAC. Each human seat is a position of the organisation, filled through the organisation's assignment mechanism. Each has its own Chief of Staff, which is both a translator and an assistant. It turns the speed and the language of AI into the speed and the language of the human, and with the seat's approval it can do the seat's work (DR-0011).
   4. It never locks the organisation. Any part, down to the smallest Cell, can change from AI to a human seat, or back. The standard list decides what must stay human. The design stays AI-native whoever fills a part.
   5. Its aim is a successful organisation (item 1).
6. **Every part named and linked.** The introduction names every part of 49GAION where it explains it: the AIOC, the Cell, the Pod, the Chief of Staff (COS), the Office, the AI hub, the HOAC and its three kinds, and the Human Floor. Each name links to the page that defines it today. When each part gets a page of its own, only its link changes.
