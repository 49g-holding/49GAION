# DR-0037 · 49GAION — Live: the design of an organisation is given to AI

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the directions of the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION of 2026-10-22 (translated):
  - "In the view of 49GAION, one important part of being AI-native is that the AI itself is live. Why? Because AI grows and improves so fast that even if you design or build an AI system today, when you wake up tomorrow many better and newer versions have come."
  - "So we no longer count as effective the old structures, in which a body upstream designs and configures a whole system, and the system is then installed in a new organisation. This is one of the changes between before AI and after AI: whatever is configured elsewhere and then installed in a system we count as not enough, because it soon falls behind and grows weak."
  - "Its strategy is to give the design and configuration of the organisation itself to AI."
  - "Wherever someone may think that Edition Maker is weak or thin, know that this is its philosophy: in place of limits, it puts the AI itself. It leaves what follows the install to the live AI, gives open access to the repository, and so keeps the system stable and current."
  - "Do not delete the earlier definitions; merge these with them and add them."
  - "What organisations want to reach through 49GAION is the philosophy of access to what is live: a movement of growth whose output, whose property, is that it brings AI-native into the organisation."

## Decision

1. **AI-native is also live.** AI improves so fast that a system designed and configured today is already behind tomorrow, when better versions arrive. So the pre-AI way is not enough: a body upstream designs and configures a whole system, and the system is then installed in a new organisation. Whatever is configured elsewhere and installed as it is soon falls behind.
2. **The design and configuration of the organisation are given to AI.** The organisation's `[ORG]AION` designs and configures it, and keeps it current as AI improves, with open access to the 49GAION Library. It proposes, and a human seat decides (HF-01). This keeps the organisation both current and stable: current, because its own AI keeps its design up to date; stable, because every change still passes a human seat and must beat the version in force (Standard §2). Everything DR-0031 says stays: AI produces the output, humans increase the power of AI and cover its gaps, and a human seat is accountable for every output.
3. **The philosophy of Edition Maker.** Edition Maker does not hand over a finished design or configuration. That is its philosophy, not a gap: in place of fences, it gives the design and configuration to AI, and leaves what follows the handover to the organisation's live `[ORG]AION`.
4. **What an organisation comes for.** An organisation comes to 49GAION for access to what is live: a design that keeps pace as AI improves, and a Library that grows with what every `[ORG]AION` chooses to give back. In this, 49GAION works as a movement of growth, and what it brings into each organisation is AI-native itself.
5. **Readings.** "Built once, configured per `[ORG]AION`, never rebuilt" (Standard §1.2, row 4, and the Library register) and "Configuration, not forks" (master definition, section 3, item 11): the configuration is made inside the organisation, by its own `[ORG]AION`, live. A part of the Library is a starting point, never a finished configuration installed as it is.
