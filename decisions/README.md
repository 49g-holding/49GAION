# Decision records

Each decided subject is one `DR-####` file in this folder, numbered in order and listed here. This folder holds the only copy of each decision record of 49GAION; the companion repositories link here. The status of each record is kept only in this index.

**Frozen records (DR-0006).** A record file on main never changes. A new record takes the next free number in merge order. A row of this index changes only in its Status cell: from proposed to in force, or from in force to superseded by a newer record. A required check on main enforces this.

## Reading notes

- Records written before v1.1.0 may name a person and carry a Status line of their own. The index above governs status; those records are not edited.
- DR-0002 item 1, "Any unit, AIOC or HOAC, may be composed of smaller units of either kind": superseded (DR-0004): an AIOC may hold other AIOCs and its own Pods and Cells; HOAC is a label, not a unit.
- Decisions are recorded under "the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION".
- A record from before v1.5.0 (`DR-0001`) names a filler of a seat. Records are frozen and stay as written (DR-0006); from v1.5.0 on, fillers are kept in the private holders register, not in records or the unit register (DR-0007).
- DR-0007 item 8, 'outside every AIOC', is read with the root rule (Standard §1.7, item 13): the gateway is held in the Charter, outside every other AIOC.
- DR-0008: Read with DR-0009 (signing delegation, gateway reading, after-the-fact review of OCO's commits).
- DR-0008 item 2, "the first part an organisation receives after the Charter", is read with Protocol §13, item 1: "with the GOV, TM and OCO installed in it" reads as "with `[ORG]HOAC-GOV`, `[ORG]HOAC-TM` and `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` installed in it".
- DR-0008 item 5, "the joining test": defined in Standard §3.2, item 4, and Protocol §11, item 1.
- DR-0011 Decision item 3, the list of seventeen arrangement models: superseded (DR-0012): three models remain (Solo, Team, Overseen); the other names are withdrawn, not renamed.
- DR-0013, "a natural person does not exist" and "no natural person": read with DR-0015, item 1: no natural person is an actor of the system, and every human is present in it as its position, a legal persona; the natural person behind it exists, known only to top management.
- DR-0007 Decision item 2, "one person holding two seats in the same body counts once": superseded (DR-0015): each member Cell of a body is its own party and counts once.
- DR-0007 Decision item 11 (c), compensating controls, as far as it makes a control a rule for fewer distinct people than a rule needs: superseded (DR-0015): two positions are two parties; a compensating control is top management's own choice, and no rule, gateway or check reads it.
- DR-0007 Decision item 7, "a live, phishing-resistant sign-in of one person": read with DR-0015, item 7: the sign-in proves only that the signer is a current occupant of the signing position.
- DR-0010 Decision item 3, the lines it added that count persons (Standard §4.1, item 13, the load line per person; §4.4, item 16, one person under two seats counts once): superseded (DR-0015): load and coverage are measured per position, and two seats are two parties.
- DR-0001 Decision item 1, "and the AION Builder": superseded (DR-0032): the AION Builder is withdrawn, and 49GAION has five parts.
- DR-0007 Decision item 10, the delivery test line D-4: superseded (DR-0032): the delivery test is withdrawn with all its lines; the rules it checked stay in force on their own, and the rule of D-4 is kept in Standard §1.7, item 12.
- DR-0007 Decision item 11 (c), "and the delivery test result points to those events": superseded (DR-0032), with the delivery test.
- DR-0032, "49GAION has five parts": superseded (DR-0035): part 6 is 49GAION Edition Maker, and 49GAION has six parts.
- DR-0033: withdrawn in full (DR-0034).
- DR-0002 Decision item 8, "a derived Standard is identical to the 49GAION Standard or stricter, never contradictory": read with DR-0035: it holds at the handover; after it, the organisation's Standard is its own.
- DR-0034 item 3 and DR-0035, "branded AION" and "the branded ones": read as `[ORG]AION` (DR-0036). The organisation's AION has no other name.
- DR-0031 (AI-native) and DR-0035 (Edition Maker): read with DR-0037, which adds that an AI-native organisation is also live: its design and configuration are given to AI, inside it.
- DR-0035 item 2, "Who starts there": corrected (DR-0038): every order or request made to 49GAION is processed by Edition Maker, which belongs to 49GAION itself; what an organisation receives is its own `[ORG]AION`.
- DR-0035 item 2, "Its requirements are kept by the governing seat, outside this repository": corrected (DR-0039): they live in the 49GAION Library, in the Protocol repository, at `library/edition-maker/`.
- DR-0035, "how the core relates to the root and to the AI hub is each organisation's own design": settled (DR-0041): the core is the root, made from the Library part 49GAION Charter, and the Top Management Group is the hub of the operation, human and AI.
- DR-0007 and DR-0029, "the Charter holds the human Cells that carry `[ORG]HOAC-GOV` and `[ORG]HOAC-TM`": read with DR-0041: the root holds the governance seat; the Top Management position sits in the Top Management Group, the first AIOC under the core.
- Every record that names `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` as the AI hub through which every AIOC joins: read with DR-0041: the Top Management Group is the hub of the operation; a separate `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`, where an organisation has one, serves the operation under Top Management.
- DR-0039, the approval of Edition Maker's requirements: confirmed by the governing seat on 2026-10-25 ("Yes, I set Edition Maker."), recorded in DR-0042, item 8.
- DR-0041, item 3, and the complete definition, "the few safeguards that always hold" and "one engine writes, two challenge": read with DR-0042, items 1 and 5.
