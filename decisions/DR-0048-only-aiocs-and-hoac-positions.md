## Decision

1. **Only AIOCs and HOAC positions (the definition, §1 item 5).**
   - The system recognises two kinds of actor and no other:
     - AI, organised in AIOCs, with each AI part recorded with its role and its duties;
     - human positions, each a legal position carrying a HOAC label, from the governance seat to a single Cell.
   - No natural person, and no person's name, is recorded in the system. Authority rests with the position.
2. **The organisation's own mechanism for its human side (§6).**
   - Every human role is recorded by its legal position, never as an individual.
   - Within the system, a human position is defined only by:
     - its nature, which is human, so no AI fills it;
     - its stated duties.
   - Each organisation maintains an organisational mechanism of its own. It determines how the legal positions are assigned and administered, and which positions natural persons hold, including successors, deputies and boards.
   - The mechanism also issues the credentials with which a position's signatures are made. The system verifies the credential for the position, never the person.
   - The mechanism operates within the law and outside the system. 49GAION does not define it. Ultimate authority over it derives from the owner and is kept by governance (§15, capability 2, item 5).
   - The governance seat is a single position, `[ORG]HOAC-GOV` (§15, capability 2, item 1; §18).
   - Wording that put a person where a position belongs is brought into line in §6 item 3, §10, §13, §14, §16, §18 and §20.
3. **The Library's two sections (§9).**
   - The two sections are Parts and Products, as DR-0025 set them.
   - An organisation's own Library is set out the same way.
4. **Names (§1 item 2; Part F).**
   - Letter case does not make a different name; every other character does.
   - An organisation's `[ORG]AION` is never 49GAION. No organisation code may be used that makes it read as 49GAION in any letter case.
5. **The licence (§11 item 5).** DR-0045 is stated in the definition.
6. **Privacy (§12 item 4).** Nothing of an `[ORG]AION` is published unless the organisation chooses to publish it or the law requires it.
7. **No separate AI hub (§19).**
   - 49GAION requires no separate AI hub unit.
   - Where an organisation has one, it serves the operation under Top Management, as DR-0041, item 3.4, decided.
8. **One name (§10 item 1).** "An AION" becomes "an `[ORG]AION`" (DR-0036).
9. **Clean.** The superseded wording is listed verbatim at the end of the definition, as DR-0047 sets.
10. **Version.**
