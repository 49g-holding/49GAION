# DR-0045 · 49GAION — the owner and the licence of the public texts

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the final check of the public repositories of 25 October 2026 (no licence anywhere; no named owner); the governing seat's words of 25 and 26 October 2026 (translated): the owner of the intellectual property is the group's holding company; "settle the licence yourself, then come back." The record is prepared by the AI hub; the governing seat's merge is the decision.
- **Relation:** adds `LICENSE.md` to every public repository of 49GAION. It changes no rule of the framework and removes nothing.

## Decision

1. **The owner.** The copyright in every text of the public repositories of 49GAION is held by the group's holding company, which `LICENSE.md` names. The framework texts themselves stay free of organisation names; the licence file is the one place that names the holder, and the `no-org-names` check exempts it for that reason.
2. **The texts** are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). The credit asked for is the line "Powered by 49GAION" with a link to the hub. This is the legal floor under the complete definition's own rule on the line (`49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md`, "Powered by 49GAION"), which asks more of an organisation that takes an item of 49GAION.
3. **The code** (the scripts and workflows) is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.
4. **The names and marks** of 49GAION are not licensed. The line says where a work comes from, not how the organisation that made it runs (the complete definition, DR-0035, DR-0042).
5. **Where it is written.** `LICENSE.md`, identical, in the hub, the Protocol, the AIOC repository and the eight mother repositories; one line in each README.
6. **What cannot be undone.** A copy taken under these licences stays licensed; a later change of licence binds only later versions.
7. **Version.** Unreleased; the labels stay at `v1.8.0`.
