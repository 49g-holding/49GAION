## Decision

1. **The guide is approved.** The 49GAION Guide, Version 2, is the text of `guide/THE-49GAION-GUIDE-VERSION-2.md`. It replaces Version 1 whole; Version 1 stays in the history of this repository, at the release `v2.0.0`.
2. **What changed.** Only the places where Version 1 no longer read with the complete definition `v2.1.0`:
   - **The governance seat** is a single position. Whether a board, a board's representative or the owner stands behind it is decided by the organisation's own mechanism, outside the system (section 4; section 13).
   - **Who decides what** (section 7) opens with the system's two kinds of actor, AI in AIOCs and human positions, and the organisation's own mechanism for its human side; the final authority over that mechanism comes from the owner and is kept by the governance seat. The governance seat no longer "appoints Top Management"; it hands the operation to the Top Management position by a mandate, and Top Management sets up the positions below it.
   - **Sizes** (section 11): "from a single position to a group of companies".
   - **The Library** (section 3) names its two sections, Parts and Products (DR-0025).
   - **Where 49GAION stands** (section 12): the definition `v2.1.0`, approved on 28 October 2026 and read cold again the same day (`references/2026-10-28-test-cold-read-v2-1-raw.md`, added with this record); the repositories brought into line with it (DR-0049).
3. **What it is, and is not.** As DR-0043, item 2: the guide explains the definition and decides nothing; where they differ, the definition governs.
4. **Where it lives.** In `guide/`. Its PDF, set as Version 1 was, is published on the hub's release page with the release `v2.1.0`, as `The-49GAION-Guide-Version-2.pdf`, and cited in `guide/README.md` by its SHA-256.
5. **Links.** `README.md` and `49GAION.md` point to Version 2.
6. **Version.** Unreleased `v2.1.0`, with DR-0048 and DR-0049. The labels and `state.json` stay at `v2.0.0`.
