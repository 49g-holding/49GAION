# DR-0013 · 49GAION — no natural person: every human is a position

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the direction of the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION of 2026-10-15: "In the AION system the human as a natural person does not exist at all. Every human that exists, exists as a legal persona. Where legal personas are linked to natural persons, that has its place only in top management, belongs to it, has its own mechanism, and has nothing to do with you. Write it in the Standard, in an important and clear place, with emphasis, so that this discussion is closed." (translated)
- **Relation:** builds on DR-0011 (the Position Principle) and DR-0012; it supersedes nothing. Both stay as merged.
- **Number:** this record takes the next free number at merge, after DR-0012

## Decision

1. **No natural person.** In 49GAION and in every `[ORG]AION`, a natural person does not exist. A human exists in the system only as a position: a legal persona with its own code, carrying a HOAC label. Every human act in the system (deciding, signing, approving, answering, contributing, being accountable) is the act of a position.

2. **Nowhere a natural person.** No Blueprint, card, Work Item, register entry read by a part, record, prompt, instruction or AI memory names, addresses, describes or reasons about a natural person who acts in the system.

3. **The link is top management's alone.** The link between a position and the natural persons who occupy it belongs only to `[ORG]HOAC-TM`, under `[ORG]HOAC-GOV`, in its own mechanism: the arrangement of each position, and the occupancy and handover entries of the private register copy (DR-0011, Decision item 4; DR-0012). For `[ORG]HOAC-TM` itself, GOV holds that link; for GOV, the founding configuration.

4. **No AI part touches it.** No AIOC, Pod, Cell, bot or other AI part holds, reads, writes, stores or infers that link. An AI part addresses a position, through that position's HU port; whoever occupies the position under the arrangement in force receives it there. Only the tool gateway checks, at sign-in and at signing, that a live sign-in belongs to a current occupant, and it writes the occupant IDs of a decision stamp into top management's records alone.

5. **Content is not an actor.** The rule is about who acts in the system. An organisation's own sources may name people as their content; that content stays data, under its classification and the restricted partition, and never becomes an actor of the system.

6. **Where it sits.** The Standard carries the rule in a callout at its head, in §1.1 (item 9, the definition of 49GAION), in a reading note in §4.1 and in the terms table. Every other text of 49GAION, its companions (the Operating Protocol, the Naming Standard, the AIOC definition and template) and every Blueprint are read with it: wherever a text says "person", "holder", "filler" or "occupant" of a human seat, it names a position and its occupancy, which is top management's own mechanism. The Standard governs its companions (its opening paragraph).

7. **Version.** The texts sit on main under the unreleased `v1.6.0`. The version labels and `state.json` stay at `v1.5.0` until GOV releases. Any CI check for this rule goes in its own `.github` pull request and becomes required only after it runs green on main.
