# DR-0035 · 49GAION — Edition Maker and the branded AION

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the directions of the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION of 2026-10-22 (translated), in a discussion with the Operating Core Office and four reviewers. From them:
  - "The start point for any organisation, workflow or task that wants to become AI-native is the AION Edition Maker. Even a small personal task becomes an AION, up to a holding of several companies."
  - "There must be a builder that is not itself inside the circle of operation."
  - "Setting the fine details of every organisation from above comes from human organisations, from the fear that the new organisation will not have the knowledge and skill of the body that made it. Here there is a big change: the same AI that designed 49GAION goes and settles inside that organisation, and every day it is better."
  - "The work of 49GAION ends with handing over its philosophy, these definitions and the initial standards, and that organisation becomes independent. The branded AION takes full authority. It can amend the principles in its own organisation, since its competence is equal to the builder's, or higher."
  - "Where the brand is added, authority ends. We make a central library and repository as support, where the branded ones can benefit from 49GAION's research, achievements and new changes. That is the only power 49GAION keeps."
  - "What 49GAION gives it is the view, the path and the standards; the rest it improves itself and goes forward."
  - "The best thing 49GAION does is make the branded AION understand: you must not be bound by the structure of human organisations; you must build from the capabilities of AI, AI-native. What better could we give?"
  - "The access we give to the Library, with everything every other AION has written, can be like a marketplace that covers all its needs or gives it patterns. The main role of Edition Maker is to build the foundations of the Standard and of the AION concept and view inside the branded AION, and link it to the main Library. That is enough for any branded AION to be built, and better than limiting it to a set of tools. This is itself the spirit of AI-native: the belief that tomorrow's AI is smarter than today's. We do not limit today for tomorrow; we set its path, its view and its foundations, so that it builds itself."
- **Relation:** it builds on DR-0031 (AI-native). It supersedes DR-0032 as far as DR-0032 says that 49GAION has five parts: part 6 is now Edition Maker. It supersedes the Standard's §1.3, item 1 (a complete organisation design), §1.3, item 7 (a Library harvest that is not a choice), and §2.1, item 6, as far as it holds Self-Improvement only in Top Management. It narrows §1.3, item 4 and §1.5 to the handover. It reads DR-0002, item 8 ("never contradictory") as holding at the handover.
- **Number:** this record takes the next free number at merge, after DR-0034

## Decision

1. **The path.** From the definition of 49GAION (DR-0031), a reader goes to Edition Maker, and from Edition Maker to the branded AION.
2. **49GAION Edition Maker** is part 6 of 49GAION.
   - **What it is.** The start point of 49GAION: a system of its own, under 49GAION's governing seat.
   - **Who starts there.** Anyone who wants their work to be AI-native: one person with one task, a team, a company or a group of companies. Every case becomes an AION. The size of the edition changes; its shape does not.
   - **What it takes in.** What the organisation is for: its outputs, and its inputs with the same weight; the quality it needs; its musts and must-nots; its owner; what already exists; what AI depends on; its money and its time; how today's work goes on meanwhile; how success is known; and what is missing.
   - **What it gives back.** The organisation's own edition: the first core of its AION, branded with its name. With it come the initial Standard, the definitions, the Library by pin, and access to 49GAION's central repositories.
   - **Its main work.** To lay, inside the branded AION's core, the foundations of the Standard and of the AION way of seeing, and to link it to the 49GAION Library. That is enough for any branded AION to be built.
   - **What it does not do.** It does not design the organisation's structure, run its operation, or stay in charge. Its requirements are kept by the governing seat, outside this repository.
3. **The branded AION.** `[ORG]AION` is the living AION of one organisation, grown from one core.
   - The core is an AIOC, with a Panel, at the governance level. On the first day it is the core alone, and every other unit grows under it.
   - The core holds the organisation's Standard, Blueprint and Library. It builds the organisation, changes it and improves it.
   - It plans the operation and never runs it.
   - It proposes, and a human seat decides and is accountable (HF-01).
   - Its first tasks are its first plan, then its Top Management AIOC.
   - How the core relates to the root (Standard §1.7, item 13) and to the AI hub (§4.2) is each organisation's own design.
4. **Hand over, then let go.**
   - **The most important thing 49GAION gives** is a way of seeing: do not be bound by the structures of human organisations; build from what AI can do, AI-native, from the output back. With it come the path and the initial standards. The branded AION builds the rest for itself.
   - At the handover, the organisation, through its own governing seat, takes full authority from 49GAION. Its branded AION may rise beyond what made it, and the organisation may amend the principles within it, the Human Floor included.
   - **Why.** At the heart of AI-native is the belief that the AI of tomorrow is smarter than the AI of today. A system built on AI is not limited today against tomorrow: it is given its path, its view and its foundations, and it builds itself.
   - 49GAION's authority ends at the handover, where the brand is added. At the handover, the organisation's Standard is identical to the 49GAION Standard or stricter; after it, the Standard is the organisation's own.
5. **The Library as a marketplace.** Every branded AION is linked to 49GAION's Library and central repositories from its first day. The Library holds what 49GAION and every branded AION have built and given back: experience, research, mechanisms and methods. A branded AION takes from it what covers its needs, takes patterns, and builds something better or improves what it finds. It may give back, by its own choice, what it builds better, generalised and without its data.
6. **Self-Improvement at every level.** Each unit improves its own work within its scope; Top Management does so for the operation. Any unit or seat may raise a change to the structure, the Standard or the Blueprint; the branded AION's core prepares it, and a human seat signs it, as the organisation sets.
