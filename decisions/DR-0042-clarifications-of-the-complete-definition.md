# DR-0042 · 49GAION — clarifications of the complete definition

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the review notes of the plain-language guide (draft 0.3) that the governing seat attached on 2026-10-25, with five contradictions found in the complete definition and fifteen suggestions; the clarifications proposed for them; and the governing seat's approval of the proposal the same day (translated): "Yes."
- **Relation:** it adds to DR-0041. It removes nothing from the complete definition: each clarification is a labelled addition or reading marked DR-0042 in `49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md`. Where it narrows a rule, it says so below.
- **Number:** this record takes the next free number at merge, after DR-0041

## Decision

1. **"Always."** Where the complete definition says that something always holds, it means: in every edition 49GAION hands over. After the handover the principles, the Human Floor and the safeguards are the organisation's own, within the law.
2. **The live sign-in and a standing approval.** A fresh live sign-in (HF-10) is needed for each signature; "every approval" reads "every signature". A standing approval is signed once, with a fresh live sign-in, and not again for each use under it; it covers only changes that can be undone and do not widen access, budget or authority; every widening needs a fresh signature.
3. **A Chief of Staff orders and summarises, never drops.** It never drops or delays what reaches its human position, and the raw record is always open to that position. The protected route of a report, an objection or a stop never passes through any AI, the core included.
4. **The gateway and the stop beside the core.** They are installed with the core but run as code beside it, outside the reach of every AI, the core's own included. The core's own stop is held directly by the governance seat.
5. **One engine is never enough.** The safeguard is that no step runs on one engine: no result counts without a challenge by another engine, scaled to the risk of the step. "One engine writes, two challenge" reads as the default pattern of the Library, no longer a fixed rule for every organisation; in Edition Maker it stays mandatory at every step.
6. **Eight further principles.**
   1. Any change to the code of the gateway or the stop counts as loosening: a human signature, made by someone other than the AI it constrains.
   2. At the handover the organisation issues fresh gateway keys, and every key Edition Maker made, held or used is revoked.
   3. Every change to the organisation's rules is recorded with the deciding position, its own words, the reason and the version it replaced.
   4. In every draft, the deciding position's own words are kept apart from text written by AI; nothing undecided is recorded as approved.
   5. Success is proven when the approved measures hold, from the record of the operation, for a period the governance seat sets, as checked by someone other than the core.
   6. A unit's first fixed tests are built from the quality standard taken in at the intake, marked provisional and in place before its first day of work, and replaced from real outputs.
   7. The governance seat's own conflict of interest: the founding configuration names in advance either an independent position that decides in its place or a second, independent signature the decision also needs; each recusal is reported to the internal-audit line (HF-19); a conflicted seat signing alone is refused at the gateway.
   8. The authority ceiling: every work item carries its original requester; an act runs only within the authority of both the unit that does it and the original requester, however it is routed.
7. **The order of precedence.** The complete definition first, then the Standard with the Naming Standard, then every other text of 49GAION.
8. **Edition Maker's requirements.** The governing seat confirmed on 2026-10-25 that it approved them ("Yes, I set Edition Maker."). The confirmation is the source of their approval, read with DR-0039.
9. **The tests.** The two refusals of item 6 (a conflicted seat signing alone; a request beyond the original requester's authority) are written as must-fail cases in the fixed test of the Library part 49GAION Charter, which holds the gateway.
10. **Version.** Unreleased. The version labels and `state.json` stay at `v1.7.0` until GOV releases.
