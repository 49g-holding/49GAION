# DR-0015 · 49GAION — the human rule: every human is present as a legal persona; two positions are two parties

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the directions of the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION of 2026-10-15. First: "HOACs are, for you, all legal personas whose nature, whose essence, is a human. We do not know them by the name of the person; you know them by their legal character. For you it is entirely enough that one legal persona differs from another. Two HOACs are two different parties for you: if HOAC CTO and HOAC DevOps sign, for you they are two different signers, even if one human stands behind both." Then: "In all of 49GAION, regarding a HOAC or a human, only the legal persona is recognised; the only place that links legal personas to natural persons is the human hub, the system's top management; nowhere else is it needed. How top management maps natural persons to legal personas and follows their responsibility, it writes itself. Write it in the master definition, the Protocol and the Standard so it does not repeat." Then, on reading DR-0013: "Write this human rule in 49GAION, and write it complete. It is not that the human does not exist; the legal position exists." (translated)
- **Relation:** builds on DR-0011 (the Position Principle), DR-0012 (the arrangement models) and DR-0013 (no natural person), and gathers the human rule in one record. It reads DR-0013 as item 1 says. It supersedes only the lines listed in item 3, each of which counts or compares the natural persons behind positions. DR-0007, DR-0010, DR-0013 and every other record stay as merged and in force in every other part; their files are not changed.
- **Number:** this record takes the next free number at merge, after DR-0014

## Decision

1. **The human is present, as a legal persona.** It is not that the human does not exist in the system. In 49GAION and in every `[ORG]AION`, every human is present, and decides, signs, approves, answers, contributes and is accountable, as a position: a legal persona with its own code, carrying a HOAC label, whose nature is human. The system knows it only by its legal character, never by the natural person behind it. That natural person exists too; it stays outside the system's knowledge, and only top management knows it (item 4). DR-0013's "a natural person does not exist" and "no natural person" are read in this sense: no natural person is an actor of the system, and every human is present in it as its position.

2. **Two positions, two parties.** In 49GAION and in every `[ORG]AION`, it is enough that one position, a legal persona, differs from another. Two positions are two parties, and two different signers, for every rule that needs more than one party: two signers, a type marked dual, maker and checker, segregation of duties (HF-18), the members, quorum and majority of a decision rule, the signer pair that a risk label selects, and recusal. This holds even when one natural person occupies both positions. No AIOC, Pod, Cell, bot, check or other AI part compares, counts or matches the natural persons behind positions. One position acting twice is still one party.

3. **Lines superseded.** Each line below counts or compares natural persons. It stays in place, marked with a reading note, and is read in position terms as follows.
   - Standard §4.1, item 10, and DR-0007, Decision item 2: "one person holding two seats in the same body counts once" reads "each member Cell of the body is its own party and counts once".
   - Standard §4.1, item 12 (acting and absence): "The acting filler signs with their own identity, under that Cell's code" and "A filler who is away without cover is recorded absent" read as dated events of the position (acting-start, acting-end, absent, returned); the signature is the position's, made with a live sign-in that belongs to a current occupant of that position (item 7).
   - Standard §4.1, item 13 (DR-0010): "every person a load line set in the `[ORG]AION`; a person above the line is flagged, also before being named to a seat" reads "load and coverage are measured per position"; any load line on the people behind positions is top management's own (item 4).
   - Standard §4.4, item 5, and Protocol §4.10, item 3: "An approval given twice by the same person, under two Cells, where two people are required is flagged and does not count as dual approval" reads "two approvals under two Cells are approvals of two parties and count as two".
   - Standard §4.4, item 8, and Protocol §4.9, item 5: "A type marked dual needs two distinct persons, neither of them the requester" reads "two distinct positions, neither of them the requesting position".
   - Standard §4.4, item 12, and DR-0007, Decision item 11 (c), as far as they make a compensating control a rule for "fewer distinct people than a rule needs", and the sentence "Where one person fills both the GOV and the TM Cell, that person signs once under each Cell code, counts once under HF-18, and the rules that need two people run under such a control": GOV and TM are two parties and each counts; a compensating control is top management's own choice (item 6).
   - Standard §4.4, item 16 (DR-0010): "one person under two seats counts once (HF-18)" reads "each of the two seats is its own party".
   - The unit register form (`registers/HOAC-REGISTER.md`): "When one person fills several human Cells, each signature is recorded with that Cell's code, and dual approval by the same person is flagged" keeps its first part; the flag on dual approval by the same person is superseded.
   - Protocol §4.10, item 13: the reference organisation "one person filling GOV and TM under a compensating control" passes with or without the control; the must-fail organisation "one person in GOV and TM without a control" no longer fails; signature sets check dual signature by distinct positions other than the requesting position.
   - Protocol §4.10, item 20: "must resolve to at least one real person" reads "must resolve to at least one occupant", which the arrangement records as a holder count of at least one; who occupies the position stays in top management's own record.
   - The description of `decision_rule` in the unit card schema, and the AIOC definition (`docs/CELLS-AND-PODS.md`, §2, item 6): "one person holding two seats in the same body counts once" reads as in the first line of this list.

4. **The people behind positions are top management's.** The link between each position and the natural persons who occupy it, the arrangement of each position, and the acting, absence, load and coverage of those persons, are the own mechanism and record of `[ORG]HOAC-TM`, the human hub, under `[ORG]HOAC-GOV` (DR-0011, Decision items 2 and 4; DR-0012; DR-0013, Decision item 3). For `[ORG]HOAC-TM` itself, GOV holds that link; for GOV, the founding configuration. How top management maps natural persons to positions and follows their responsibility, it writes itself; 49GAION fixes none of it. Nowhere else is the link needed: no AI part holds, reads, writes, stores or infers it, and the AI side measures load, coverage, acting and absence per position only. A natural person may appear in the system only as an organisation's own content, never as an actor (DR-0013, Decision item 5).

5. **Records stay with the position.** Every act is recorded under the position that took it, and stays with that position when its occupant changes, as an AI Cell's record stays its own when the engine or model behind it changes. Everything the position carries passes in full on a change of occupant (DR-0011, the Position Principle).

6. **Compensating controls are top management's own choice.** A compensating control for one occupant behind several positions is top management's own choice, in its own mechanism. No rule of 49GAION requires it, and neither the tool gateway nor any check, the delivery test included, reads, requires or counts it. A control that top management records still never lowers the Human Floor, never applies to HF-12 and never accepts an AI signature.

7. **The gateway compares nothing else.** The tool gateway checks only that a live sign-in belongs to a current occupant of the signing position (DR-0013, Decision item 4), against top management's own record, and applies each position's arrangement as top management recorded it (DR-0012). Every other signature rule it applies is a rule on positions: the signing Cell and its authority scope, the number of distinct signing positions, the requesting position and recusal. It never compares the occupants behind two positions; two signatures under two positions count as two even when one occupant made both. The pseudonymous identity reference of a signature record serves the sign-in check and top management's records; no rule counts or matches it. "A live, phishing-resistant sign-in of one person" (DR-0007, Decision item 7; Protocol §4.10, item 10) is read in this sense.

8. **Where it sits.** The Standard carries the rule in a callout at its head, beside DR-0013's, in §1.1, item 10, with a reading note on item 9, reading notes at each superseded line of item 3, and rows of the terms table. The master definition carries it in §3, item 8. The unit register form and the index of decision records carry reading notes. The Operating Protocol carries reading notes at §4.9, item 5, and §4.10, items 3, 10, 13 and 20, and in the descriptions of `decision_rule` and `signer_identity_ref`; its tests count positions, never persons. The AIOC definition and template carry reading notes at `docs/CELLS-AND-PODS.md`, §2, item 6, and at the template rows on positions, the load line and the role card. The companions mark these with the dated direction of 15 October 2026 until this record is on main. Every other text of 49GAION, its companions and every Blueprint are read with it.

9. **Version.** The texts sit on main under the unreleased `v1.6.0`. The version labels and `state.json` stay at `v1.5.0` until GOV releases. Any CI check for this rule goes in its own `.github` pull request and becomes required only after it runs green on main.
