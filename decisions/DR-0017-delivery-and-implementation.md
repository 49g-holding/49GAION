# DR-0017 · 49GAION — Delivery and Implementation, the stage that takes the design into a running organisation

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the directions of the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION of 2026-10-15, on the product requirements drafted by the AI hub and reviewed by three independent reviewer seats: "Approve it as an AIOC and a part of the Library. Its name stays Delivery and Implementation. Start the first slice." Earlier the same day, on what it is: an assistant that holds the whole design and accompanies the organisation in delivering it; it diagnoses the implementation and finds the gaps in a design made elsewhere; after go-live it optimises and improves, monitors the processes, builds the measures and reports, proposes processes and job descriptions, and mentors; it takes input from any source, most precisely from the target operating model and Process and Position Design, and also serves small and one-person businesses. And: "We have started a human process; bring it to its end." (translated)
- **Relation:** builds on DR-0013, DR-0014, DR-0015 and DR-0016; it supersedes nothing.
- **Number:** this record takes the next free number at merge, after DR-0016

## Decision

1. **The part.** **49GAION Delivery and Implementation** is a Library part, with its card in `49GAION-protocol` (`library/cards/delivery-and-implementation.part-card.json`, `DRAFT`, `last_result` `NOT_RUN`) and its rules in the Protocol section of the same name. In an `[ORG]AION` it is the AIOC `[ORG]AIOC-DI`. A part of its own.
2. **Its place.** The last stage of the design chain: it takes the decided design into a running organisation, and stays to run and improve it with the organisation.
3. **Three ways in.** Mode A: the decided releases of the target operating model and of Process and Position Design, by reference and hash. Mode B: a design made elsewhere, filed as a source in Source Lifecycle. Mode C: no design; it builds a light design with the owner, every element provisional until decided.
4. **What it does.** Diagnose (the gap check, in two tiers: what blocks launch, and what is incomplete); prepare (procedures, drafts of policies and authority instruments, the control matrix, the obligations register, the transition plan and the go-live criteria); accompany (the plan, answers grounded in the design, problem records, learning paths, readiness, rehearsals and the go or no-go package); operate (the operating rhythm and its logs, measures and forum packs, version control, acknowledgement, runnable checklists, quarterly priorities, the value register); improve (findings and proposals at process level, trials, the health check, and the hand-over of its skills to the organisation).
5. **Who decides.** It decides nothing for the organisation. A permission ladder per class of action: read; propose (a draft enters a record only when a position inserts it); apply within a standing approval, only where reversible and with no legal, pay or employment effect. No level allows an item of the Human Floor signature list. Any accountable position can stop it, or any change it applied, at once.
6. **Never about persons.** It works at the level of processes and units. It never ranks, scores or watches the occupant of a position, and shows no measure for a single position. Watching a process starts only after the organisation records its impact assessment and informs workers or their representatives where law requires (DR-0015).
7. **Drafts with legal effect.** Each records its entity and jurisdiction, is marked as a draft and not legal advice, and goes to decision only after review by a qualified professional for that jurisdiction.
8. **Returns.** Defects it finds in a design release go back to the stage that made it under the Library's design return contract (DR-0018), and the loop ends as DR-0016, item 5 says.
9. **Where it sits.** The Standard carries the part in §3.1, item 5, and in the terms table; the Protocol in the section of the same name, with the card and its fixed test, TEST-DELIVERY-AND-IMPLEMENTATION v1. Its mother blueprint is written in its own writing repository; its product requirements in its code project. The first slice is the gap audit and the launch essentials.
10. **Version.** The texts sit on main under the unreleased `v1.6.0`. The version labels and `state.json` stay at `v1.5.0` until GOV releases.
