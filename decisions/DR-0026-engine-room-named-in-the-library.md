## Decision

1. **Named.** 49GAION Engine Room is named in the Library register as an AIOC part. In an `[ORG]AION` it is `[ORG]AIOC-ER`.
2. **What it is, in short.** The one hub of an `[ORG]AION` for every AI engine and every paid external service. Each unit draws from it through a section of its own, with its own quota. It is demand-led: a unit states its need, and the part supplies it and never decides it.
3. **The tool gateway stays in the Charter.** The part supplies the engines the tool gateway binds and never replaces the tool gateway (Standard §1.7, item 13; §4.4, item 11).
4. **Named, not yet built.** Its card, its fixed test and its mother blueprint are not yet written. The register says so. Nothing is marked `DRAFT` or tested until they are.
5. **Version.** Unreleased `v1.6.0`, as DR-0019.
