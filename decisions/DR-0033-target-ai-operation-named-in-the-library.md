# DR-0033 · 49GAION — Target AI Operation named in the Library

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the direction of the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION of 2026-10-22 (translated), on a name proposed by the Operating Core Office. From it:
  - "I want something here that first takes the data and understands what the outputs are, by the definition we made. Then, from those outputs, it designs the map and the architecture for reaching them with AI, and it also makes the requirements and the gaps that AI cannot cover."
  - "I like Target AI Operation. Record it now, and register it in the Library, among the AIOCs, as an AIOC. Open the repository. Then we go into its definition and its scope."
  - "As 49GAION now has a Standard, a Protocol and a Library, it must design the same for each unique AION: write its Standard, write its proposed Protocol, and define at least its minimal Library."
  - "It designs the branded AION. It does not enter its building."
- **Relation:** builds on DR-0019 (the Library register), DR-0025 (Parts and Products), DR-0031 (AI-native) and DR-0032 (the AION Builder withdrawn). It follows the pattern of DR-0026 (a part named before it is built). It supersedes nothing.
- **Number:** this record takes the next free number at merge, after DR-0032

## Decision

1. **Named.** 49GAION Target AI Operation is named in the Library register as an AIOC part. In an `[ORG]AION` its code is `[ORG]AIOC-TAO`.
   - **The collision check (Naming Standard §5).** `TAO` recreates neither `AIOC`, `AION` nor `49GAION`. It matches no code in use. It reads as neither a version suffix nor a misleading acronym.
2. **What it is, in short.** It starts from an organisation's outputs and what they must meet. From them, it designs that organisation's `[ORG]AION`, AI-native (DR-0031): its own Standard, its proposed Protocol and its minimal Library. It designs the `[ORG]AION`; it does not build it.
3. **Its mother repository.** `49g-holding/49GAION-target-ai-operation`, public, laid out as a mother repository (DR-0024), with `[ORG]` placeholders and no organisation's data.
4. **Named, not yet defined.** Its definition and scope, its card, its fixed test and its mother blueprint are not yet written. The register says so. Nothing is marked `DRAFT` or tested until they are. Its definition and scope are to be set with the governing seat, and checked by reviewers.
5. **Version.** Unreleased `v1.8.0`. The version labels and `state.json` stay at `v1.7.0` until GOV releases.
