# DR-0043 · 49GAION — The 49GAION Guide, Version 1

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the governing seat's direction of 2026-10-25 for a plain-language text on 49GAION version 1, written after the complete definition (DR-0041); its review notes on draft 0.3 and the clarifications they led to (DR-0042); the guide's draft 0.4, written from the definition with those clarifications; and the governing seat's word on 2026-10-25 (translated): "I agree with all of it. Do all of it; give me the PDF, and put it on GitHub too."
- **Relation:** it adds a text below the complete definition and the Standard. It changes no rule and removes nothing.

## Decision

1. **The guide is approved.** The 49GAION Guide, Version 1, is the plain-language text of 49GAION: what it is, how it works, and how an organisation starts with it. It is the draft 0.4 of 2026-10-25, as approved.
2. **What it is, and is not.** The guide explains the complete definition and decides nothing. In the order of precedence (DR-0042, item 7) it stands with every other text of 49GAION, below the complete definition and the Standard. Where the guide and the definition differ, the definition governs.
3. **Where it lives.** The text is kept in this repository, in `guide/`. Its PDF is kept outside the repository, as the `no-binaries` check requires, and is published on the hub's release page with the release that follows this record, `v1.8.0`, as `The-49GAION-Guide-Version-1.pdf`, cited in `guide/README.md` by its SHA-256.
4. **Its mark.** The guide is a text of 49GAION, so it carries no organisation's mark: the typeface Inter, the line "Powered by 49GAION", and the marks of 49GAION once they exist.
5. **How it was checked.** Three rounds of challenge by engines from three providers, each against the complete definition, and the governing seat's own review of draft 0.3, whose findings went into the definition first (DR-0042) and then into the guide.
6. **Later versions.** The guide changes only after the definition changes. A new version is approved by the governing seat and replaces the earlier one whole, by pull request, with its own record; the earlier version stays in the history.
7. **Version.** Unreleased. The version labels and `state.json` stay at `v1.7.0` until GOV releases.
