# DR-0047 · 49GAION — the clean version: `v2.0.0`, and every release written clean from now on

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the structure review of 26 October 2026, points B1 and B2 (the layered texts: the definition page contradicting itself between its fifth and ninth lines; the Standard carrying 37 additions, 33 readings and 31 superseded labels; the `no-removal` baseline of `v1.4.0` locking wording such as the count of the parts); the governing seat's words of 26 October 2026 (translated): "my confirmation was for all your proposals", among them the clean version. The record is prepared by the AI hub; the governing seat's merge is the decision.
- **Relation:** supersedes the way of changing texts set by `49GAION/DR-0006` for the texts (not for the decision records, which stay frozen): since `v1.4.0` every change was additive, the old sentence kept beside a labelled Addition or Reading. It changes no rule of 49GAION: `v2.0.0` states the meaning that `v1.8.0` already had.

## Decision

1. **`v2.0.0` is the clean version.** The hub's complete definition, master definition, Standard, Naming Standard and README; the Operating Protocol with the Library register pages; and the AIOC definition, template and docs are rewritten so that each text states its current meaning in its body. Every Addition is part of the body; every Reading has replaced the sentence it read; the wording that was superseded has left the body and is listed once, verbatim, at the end of each text under "Superseded wording, by version", with what superseded it. Each section that folded anything ends with a line naming the records it drew on.
2. **No rule changes.** No rule is added, dropped, weakened or strengthened; no heading number, rule id, Human Floor item, defined term or code changes, with one heading reworded to the truth it states (Standard §1.2, seven parts, the Blueprint among them since `49GAION/DR-0041`). The master definition (`49GAION.md`) becomes the short entry to the texts, its eight sections kept by name; the complete definition and the Standard are the one source each of what 49GAION is and of its rules (point B2 of the review).
3. **From now on each release is written clean.** A change to a text states the current meaning in the body; the wording it supersedes goes to the list at the end of the text and stays readable at the previous tag. Labelled additions and readings are no longer the way; a decided text is still never edited in place, because every release is a new version that names what it supersedes.
4. **The `no-removal` baseline moves with the release.** The baseline of `v1.4.0` guarded the texts through `v2.0.0`. After this release the check's baseline is generated from the clean texts and guards their headings, ids and terms; each later release regenerates it. That change is a `.github` change of its own, after the merge of the texts.
5. **The layered texts remain** at the tags `v1.4.0` to `v1.8.0`; the decision records are untouched (`dr-frozen`).
6. **Version.** `v2.0.0` for the hub, the Operating Protocol and the AIOC repository together; the Protocol pin of the AIOC repository and of the mother repositories moves to the Protocol's `v2.0.0` by their own pull requests.
