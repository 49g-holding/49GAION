# DR-0051 · 49GAION — the HOAC Assignment Model, kept only in the Top Management Group

- **Date:** 2026-10-29
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION
- **Source:**
  - The governing seat's ruling of 29 October 2026 (translated, in substance): "The system does not read anything; the system knows only HOAC, the positions. The HOAC Assignment Model is a feature inside the Top Management AIOC; it is defined only there. Each organisation is free to have its own model. It is recorded only there and stays only there; no other part has anything to do with it. When GOV is formed and makes this delegation of authority, the record goes there and stays there: an internal human matter, unrelated to any of the AIs. The possible models are a separate protocol for how assignment is done, with many variants: from a single human, to a hundred humans in different positions, to each position given to a group of humans. Different mechanisms, but internal."
  - The record is prepared by the AI hub; the governing seat's merge is the decision.
- **Relation:**
  - It amends DR-0048, item 2 (the organisation's own mechanism, §6 of the complete definition), as far as where the link between positions and people is kept: it is kept only inside the Top Management Group, as the HOAC Assignment Model. Everything else in DR-0048 stays, including §1 item 5: no natural person, and no person's name, is recorded in the system.
  - It supersedes DR-0041, item 3.1, as far as it says 49GAION never introduces a "one-person organisation": one person holding every position is a model 49GAION introduces and promotes, while the positions stay separate.
  - DR-0041 and DR-0048 are frozen and are read with this one.
- **Number:** this record takes the next free number at merge, after DR-0050.

## Decision

1. **The HOAC Assignment Model.** How an organisation assigns its HOAC positions to people is its HOAC Assignment Model. It is a feature of the organisation's Top Management Group, the Top Management position with its AIOC (the complete definition, §19). It is defined, recorded and kept only there.
2. **Each organisation sets its own.** 49GAION does not prescribe it. The models include one person holding every position, which 49GAION introduces and promotes; many people in different positions; and positions held by groups of people.
3. **The delegation of authority.** When the governance seat is formed and delegates authority, the record of that assignment is kept in the Top Management Group, as an internal human matter.
4. **No other part reads it.** No other AIOC, no AI and no part or check of 49GAION reads it or depends on it. 49GAION knows only HOAC positions.
5. **The positions stay separate.** The structure never has one single HOAC position that covers everything. One person may still hold all the positions.
6. **The assignment protocol.** The range of possible assignment models is a separate assignment protocol of the Top Management Group. It is future work, to be defined later; this record writes no specification for it.
7. **Where it is written.**
   - `49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md`: §6, item 4, and Part F.
   - `standards/49GAION-NAMING-STANDARD.md`: §3.
   - `upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md`: the glossary row "HOAC Assignment Model".
   - This record, its index row, its reading notes, and one unreleased line in `STATE.md`.
8. **Version.** Unreleased. The version labels and `state.json` stay at `v2.1.0` until GOV releases.
