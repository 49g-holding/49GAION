# DR-0036 · 49GAION — One name: `[ORG]AION`

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (holder in the private holders register)
- **Source:** the direction of the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION of 2026-10-22 (translated): "Make one correction: we do not have a 'branded AION'. Everywhere, make it `[ORG]AION`."
- **Relation:** it changes the wording of DR-0034 and DR-0035, not what they decide. It supersedes nothing else.
- **Number:** this record takes the next free number at merge, after DR-0035

## Decision

1. **One name.** The AION of an organisation is `[ORG]AION`, and it has no other name. Shown with the organisation's brand, it is still its `[ORG]AION` (Naming Standard R1).
2. **"Branded AION" is not a term of 49GAION.** Where DR-0034 and DR-0035 say "branded AION", or "the branded ones", read `[ORG]AION`. Those records stay as written (DR-0006).
3. **Where it is written.**
   - `49GAION.md`: section 2.5, its heading and its text; section 3, item 7; section 5, item 1.
   - The Standard: §1.1, item 3; §1.3, items 1, 4 and 7; §2.1, item 6; the glossary, where the row "Branded AION" is removed and the row "`[ORG]AION`" carries its meaning.
   - The Protocol: §10, item 4.
4. **Version.** Unreleased `v1.8.0`. The version labels and `state.json` stay at `v1.7.0` until GOV releases.
