# Contributing

**49GAION, the hub · `v2.1.1`.**

1. **Read first:** the complete definition (`49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md`), which governs where any other text differs; then `49GAION.md` and the Standard (`upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md`).
2. **One change, one pull request,** with every required check green, reviewed by a party other than its writer, and merged by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. `main` is protected; tags `v*` are locked; nothing is ever force-pushed.
3. **Decided texts are never edited.** A change to a decision record is a new record that names what it supersedes (`decisions/`, `dr-frozen`). From `v2.0.0` each release is written clean: a change states the current meaning in the body, and the wording it superseded is listed at the end of the text and kept at the previous tag (DR-0047). The `no-removal` check guards the headings, ids and terms of the current baseline.
4. **No organisation's name or data** (`no-org-names`): write 49GAION for the framework and `[ORG]AION` for its product. English only (`no-persian`). Positions, never people. Binaries stay outside the repository, cited by link and SHA-256 (`no-binaries`).
5. **A `.github/` change stands alone** in its own pull request (`github-separation`).
6. **Proposals from an `[ORG]AION`** come generalised: the current text, the proposed text, the reason and evidence, and the parts and tests they affect.
7. **Licence.** By contributing you license your contribution under the licences in `LICENSE.md`.
