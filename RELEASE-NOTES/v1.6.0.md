# 49GAION Standard v1.6.0

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decisions: twenty records, [`DR-0008`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0008-source-lifecycle-first-stage.md) to [`DR-0027`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0027-operations-event-register.md), each listed with its subject in [`decisions/README.md`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/README.md).

## Summary

### The human and the position

- Every human is present as a position: a legal persona with its own code. No natural person is an actor of the system. The link between a position and the people who occupy it belongs only to top management, and no AI part touches it (DR-0011, DR-0013, DR-0015).
- Two positions are two parties for every rule that needs more than one party. Lines that counted persons are read in position terms (DR-0015).
- Three HOAC Arrangement Models: Solo, Team and Overseen (DR-0011, DR-0012).

### Mechanisms and the gateway

- Fifty-five mechanisms adopted from practice into the Standard, the Operating Protocol and the AIOC definition. The Human Floor signature list gains two types: `sign-standing-action-class` and `dispose-records-or-data` (DR-0010).
- Signing delegation through `authority_scope`, and the reading that the tool gateway is held in the Charter (DR-0009).
- Engines at the tool gateway: a register of the engines it may bind, and a log of the engine bound and the engine that served each call. Binding an engine is gateway policy, not a change of filler (DR-0020).

### The Library

- The design chain: four Library parts, each standing alone.
  - Source Lifecycle, the first stage of every source (DR-0008).
  - Target Operating Model (DR-0016).
  - Process and Position Design (DR-0014).
  - Delivery and Implementation (DR-0017).
- The stages are joined by one design return contract and one release notice contract (DR-0018, DR-0019). They also carry strategy, delivery models, priority, value and the work as it runs (DR-0023).
- One Library register; method catalogues, with the reference-model catalogue `v0.1.0` (DR-0019, DR-0022).
- Repositories: Naming Standard R9 (visibility, recorded before it is created, mother and instance), and a mother repository for a part whose first instance holds an organisation's data (DR-0021, DR-0024).
- Built from the smallest units: the Library in two sections, Parts and Products. The first assembly is Organisation Design and Delivery (DR-0025).
- Engine Room named in the Library register (DR-0026).
- The Operations Event Register, an AIOC part, and the second assembly, Organisation Memory: Source Lifecycle and the Operations Event Register side by side (DR-0027).

## Companions

Released with this version: the 49GAION Operating Protocol `v1.6.0` (`49g-holding/49GAION-protocol`) and the AIOC definition and blueprint template `v1.6.0` (`49g-holding/49GAION-aioc`).

## Known limits

- The Library is written as a map first. Every part card is `DRAFT`, and its fixed test reads `NOT_RUN` until the part is built and run.
- Engine Room has no card, fixed test or mother blueprint yet (DR-0026, item 4).
- DR-0009, item 4 stays open: whether HF-07 needs a listed type of its own, `change-accountable-to`.
