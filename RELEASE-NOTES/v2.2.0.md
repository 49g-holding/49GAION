Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decision: [`DR-0053`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0053-the-review-rooms-designs-v2-2-0.md), on the governing seat's word of 29 October 2026 (translated from Persian): "This new work they proposed: if you agree, and it is right, and it contradicts what I want nowhere, do the real request."

## Summary

### New rules (Standard §1.8; §4.4, items 20 to 24)

- **The floor list changes only by a human.** Each listed type carries `floor` (`fixed` or `org-choice`) and may carry `quorum`. Removing a type, turning `fixed` into `org-choice`, or changing a protected sentence of the Human Floor waits for a human approval, read from the base of the change; no bot gives it. Every type of 49GAION's list is `fixed`.
- **A new credential is an event,** recorded by position and reported at once to the internal-audit line and the position above; an optional waiting period before a new GOV or TM credential signs a listed type; a stop is never delayed.
- **Outside input taints the run.** After one external read, the gateway refuses the run's sends, publications, writes outside its space, new targets and changes of permission without a deterministic check or the accountable position's approval; stored content keeps its label.
- **A signature is bound to what it signs:** by position and credential, never a claim about the signer; to its subject by hash; single-use; within its validity; signer and executor distinct.
- **One event record,** append-only and chained by hash, each decision with its basis; its form and conformance scenarios are in the Operating Protocol `v2.2.0`, in its section on the event record.
- **Conformance and proof.** A text proves nothing by itself; an implementation is conformant only when its own trace of the scenarios passes the checker; an `[ORG]AION` is proven only after one end-to-end acceptance run judged by the governance seat's measures; until then, "unverified".

### The complete definition made clearer (DR-0053, item 10)

After its cold read: a Chief of Staff signs only under a grant its position has signed; what a listed decision and the founding configuration are; who checks success and Top Management's reports (a party the governance seat names); the intake's raw sources returned and deleted at the handover; what is given back enters under the licences; no dial until the seat signs one; a pointer to `ADOPTING.md`. The test passed (`references/2026-10-29-test-cold-read-v2-2-raw.md`).

### The Chief of Staff, as the governing seat ruled (DR-0053, items 11 and 12)

- **A human position works with AI through its own Chief of Staff** and may give it any authority it holds, from preparing and approving each step to full access on its behalf, signing for it included, a listed decision included. **That never means AI decides:** whatever the Chief of Staff does under its grant is the position's own decision.
- **Each position grants only to its own Chief of Staff.** AI does not give to AI: a Chief of Staff passes access, budget or authority to another AI part only with its position's leave, written in its grant. The grant is signed with a fresh live sign-in, per action or standing, revocable at once, and recorded at the gateway.
- **The record of the HOAC Assignment Model** is kept on the human half of the Top Management Group, outside every AI; it belongs to the organisation's own mechanism for its human side, not to the records of the system.

### The checks

- **`human-floor-gate`:** a change that removes a listed type, turns `fixed` into `org-choice`, or changes the protected sentences waits for the approval of the review environment `human-floor`.
- **`dr-source`:** a new decision record names its source and a date.
- **`signature-types-floor`** reads `floor` and `quorum`.
