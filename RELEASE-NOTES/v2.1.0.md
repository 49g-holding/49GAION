# 49GAION Standard v2.1.0

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decisions: three records since `v2.0.0`, [`DR-0048`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0048-only-aiocs-and-hoac-positions.md) to [`DR-0050`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0050-the-49gaion-guide-version-2.md), each listed with its subject in [`decisions/README.md`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/README.md). Released on the governing seat's word of 28 October 2026 (translated): "As long as any work of 49GAION remains, we do not move on. Go and put all of that work right too."

## Summary

### Only AIOCs and HOAC positions (DR-0048)

- **Two kinds of actor.** The system recognises AI, organised in AIOCs, each AI part recorded with its role and its duties; and human positions, each a legal position carrying a HOAC label, from the governance seat to a single Cell. No natural person, and no person's name, is recorded in the system. Authority rests with the position.
- **The organisation's own mechanism.** Each organisation keeps a mechanism of its own for its human side, within the law and outside the system: how its positions are assigned and administered, which positions natural persons hold (successors, deputies and boards included), and the credentials with which a position's signatures are made. 49GAION does not define it. Ultimate authority over it comes from the owner and is kept by the governance seat, which is a single position.
- **Also in the definition:** the Library's two sections, Parts and Products (DR-0025); names (letter case does not make a different name, and an `[ORG]AION` is never 49GAION); the licence (DR-0045); privacy (nothing of an `[ORG]AION` is published unless the organisation chooses to or the law requires it); no separate AI hub is required; one name, `[ORG]AION`.

### Positions only: the texts brought into line (DR-0049)

- **Withdrawn,** with their terms kept in the glossary, marked withdrawn, and their former wording listed verbatim at the end of each text: the arrangement models Solo and Team and the arranging power; the register entries `arrangement`, `occupancy` and `handover`, and occupant and person IDs; the acting filler of a human Cell and the signature type `appoint-acting-filler`; deputies of human seats and signature referral; the decision rule of a body behind a label; the holders register as a store 49GAION defines; the form of a compensating control for one person behind several positions; "the governance seat appoints Top Management".
- **Kept, restated by position:** the Overseen link between positions; `succeeded_by`; the duty deputy, an AI Cell; two positions, two parties; empty seats (no credential in force); signatures made under the position's Cell code with the credential the organisation's mechanism issued for it and a fresh live sign-in, stamped with the position code and time and no reference to a person; personal signatures, which stay held until the seat signs; independence in fact (HF-19), attested by the governance seat's signature; the Position Chief of Staff.
- **The checks** read only the Overseen link between positions, and the floor of the signature list takes a type out only when its trusted removal list names it with its decision record.
- **Every public text of 49GAION** follows: the Standard, the Naming Standard, the unit register form, the signature list, the Operating Protocol, its schemas and fixtures, the Edition Maker requirements, the AIOC definition and blueprint template, and the eight Library repositories, each with its own record.
