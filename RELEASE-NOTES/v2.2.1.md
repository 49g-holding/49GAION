# 49GAION Standard v2.2.1

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decision: [`DR-0054`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0054-a-review-of-v2-2-0-corrected.md). A review of `v2.2.0` was checked against `main` on 30 October 2026; what held is corrected, and what needed the governing seat's word was put to it. No rule of the complete definition, the Standard or the Operating Protocol changes.

## Summary

- **"One-person organisation."** The sentence that 49GAION never speaks of a one-person organisation leaves the Standard (§1.1, item 9) and Edition Maker's requirements, which kept it after DR-0051: one person holding every position is a model 49GAION introduces and promotes, and the positions stay distinct.
- **No list of superseded wording.** The lists at the end of the texts are taken out, as the governing seat decided: a cold reader had taken listed wording for a rule in force. The former wording stays readable at the earlier release tags, and the record that superseded it names what changed. The complete definition changes no word of its body.
- **The Guide, Version 2,** follows the complete definition at `v2.2.0`: a listed decision runs on a valid signature of its position, its own or its own Chief of Staff's under its grant; success is checked by a party the top seat names; the sources taken in are returned and deleted at the handover; a request is brought as `ADOPTING.md` sets out. Its PDF is published again with this release.
- **Labels.** The Library register's product row reads the version of Delivery and Implementation from its pin; the entry row of the complete definition names its corrections.
- **Kept.** The governing seat keeps its ruling of 29 October 2026 on the Chief of Staff's grant (DR-0053, item 11).

## The checks

- `withdrawn-wording`: a text outside the decision records, the raw references, the release notes and the change logs fails if it uses the phrase "one-person organisation" or carries a list of superseded wording.
- `ref-check`: every relative link resolves, with no list exempt.
- `no-removal`: its baseline no longer holds the heading of the lists.
