## Summary

### AI-native

- Section 2.1 of the master definition opens with what an organisation is: what reaches and holds the capability to produce a product or a service for someone outside itself, repeatable, of high quality and not dependent on any one person (DR-0031).
- It sets out two kinds of organisation (DR-0031):
  - a human-native organisation adds AI as a tool to a design built around people and their jobs;
  - an AI-native organisation is designed from the start so that AI produces its output.
- In an AI-native organisation, humans increase the power of AI and cover its gaps inside the work. Above the work, they own the organisation, set its output, quality and limits, and answer for it. A human seat is accountable for every output (DR-0031).
- AI-first now reads as AI-native (DR-0031).
- Every part named in the introduction links to the page that defines it (DR-0031).

### The Chief of Staff

- COS is the one short form of "Chief of Staff". The Chief of Staff Cell of an AIOC takes the code `[ORG]CELL-COS<initials>` (DR-0028, Naming Standard R16).

### The Charter

- The Library holds 49GAION Charter, the part from which the root AIOC of every `[ORG]AION` is made. It holds the GOV and TM Cells and three Pods of shared services (Gateway, Records and Work), one of each per `[ORG]AION` (DR-0029).
- Five readings from the Charter mother (DR-0030):
  - the code `[ORG]CELL-GOV`;
  - the Charter's ports;
  - two acts for the signature list;
  - the Charter's own parts, signed by the GOV Cell;
  - the restart of a stopped Charter, which happens outside the `[ORG]AION`.

### The design chain

- **The state of Delivery and Implementation.** A dated bullet in §3.1, item 12 records it as written in full, slices 1 to 4, and pinned in the Library register (PR #78). The bullet gives v0.2.0; the register now pins v0.2.1.

- **The delivery test.** Section 6 of `49GAION.md` and the glossary row now name all four lines, D-1 to D-4.
- **The Standard.** Four readings are added:

  - with DR-0031, on §3.1, item 12 (the first assembly is human-native; the next product is AI-native, not simply a choice of which positions an AI fills);
  - with DR-0029, on §3.1, item 11 ("runs on its own" means with the Charter and `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`, and no other Library part);
  - on §3.1, item 14, the fourth link of Organisation Memory, as Protocol §22, item 11 states it.
