# 49GAION Standard v1.8.0

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decisions: twelve records, [`DR-0032`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0032-withdrawn-aion-builder-stages-delivery-test.md) to [`DR-0043`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0043-the-49gaion-guide-version-1.md), each listed with its subject in [`decisions/README.md`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/README.md); DR-0033 is superseded by DR-0034. Released on the governing seat's word of 2026-10-25 (translated): "I agree with all of it. Do all of it."

## Summary

### The complete definition

- **`49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md`** brings the whole of 49GAION into one place, at the level of definition, and is approved (DR-0041). Where any other text of 49GAION differs from it, the definition governs, and that text is read with it until it is brought into line. The other texts were brought into line by labelled additions and readings; nothing was removed.
- **The policy.** 49GAION writes principles and the few safeguards that hold in every edition it hands over, not procedures. The details and the way of working are decided by AI at the time (DR-0041).
- **The clarifications** (DR-0042), each a labelled addition or reading in the definition:
  - "always" means in every edition 49GAION hands over; after the handover the principles, the Human Floor and the safeguards are the organisation's own, within the law;
  - a fresh live sign-in (HF-10) is needed for each signature; a standing approval is signed once and covers only changes that can be undone and do not widen access, budget or authority;
  - a Chief of Staff orders and summarises, never drops or delays; the raw record stays open; the protected route of a report, an objection or a stop never passes through any AI;
  - the gateway and the stop run as code beside the core, outside the reach of every AI; the governance seat holds the core's own stop;
  - no step runs on one engine; "one engine writes, two challenge" is the Library's default pattern and stays mandatory at every step in Edition Maker;
  - eight further principles: changes to the gateway or the stop count as loosening; fresh keys at the handover; every rule change recorded with the deciding position's own words; those words kept apart from AI text in drafts; success proven from the record, checked by someone other than the core; tests from the first day; the governance seat's own conflict of interest; the authority ceiling.
- **The order of precedence:** the complete definition, then the Standard with the Naming Standard, then every other text (DR-0042, item 7).
- **The cold-read test** is the acceptance of the definition and of the repositories read together; its questions, readers and results are in `references/2026-10-25-test-cold-read.md` (DR-0041).

### Edition Maker and the `[ORG]AION`

- **Every request starts at 49GAION Edition Maker,** part 6 of 49GAION, which belongs to 49GAION itself. It takes in what the organisation is for and designs the first core of the organisation's `[ORG]AION`: a first AIOC at the level of the governing seat, with three stages of work (DR-0035, DR-0038).
- **One name.** The AION of an organisation is `[ORG]AION`; "branded AION" is not a term of 49GAION (DR-0036).
- **Hand over, then let go.** From the handover the organisation holds full authority; the Library is a marketplace, not a fence; Self-Improvement works at every level (DR-0035).
- **Live.** The design and configuration of an organisation are given to its own `[ORG]AION`, which keeps them current as AI improves, with open access to the Library; it proposes and a human seat decides. "Built once, configured per `[ORG]AION`" and "configuration, not forks" read accordingly (DR-0037).
- **Edition Maker's requirements** live in the Library, in the Protocol repository at `library/edition-maker/`; its approval is confirmed (DR-0039; DR-0042, item 8). Edition Maker itself is built later.

### Structural Adaptation

- **The first named capability of an `[ORG]AION`.** It proposes, at every stage of the organisation's life, the structure that keeps it in balance with its resources and its needs, by split and merge of AIOCs, never merging a part with the part that checks it, and never running the operation; a human position decides each change. Written in the Standard, §1.3, item 1, and summarised in `49GAION.md` (DR-0040).

### Withdrawn

- **The AION Builder, its six stages, Ionize, the delivery test (D-1 to D-4) and the two tests at v3** (DR-0032). The headings and terms stay in the texts, each marked withdrawn, so that no reader is sent to something that no longer exists.
- **49GAION Target AI Operation** (DR-0034); DR-0033, which named it, is superseded. What it was meant to carry is carried by Edition Maker and the `[ORG]AION`.

### The guide

- **The 49GAION Guide, Version 1,** the plain-language text of 49GAION, approved and kept in `guide/` (DR-0043). It explains the definition and decides nothing. Its PDF is an asset of this release, `The-49GAION-Guide-Version-1.pdf`, SHA-256 `737fd7ce73cbfe1ad409d9788ac4c05fb2402afaca9136d514b9b3c6fb78ca85`.

## Checks on main

- **`no-binaries`:** a binary file is never added to the repository; it is kept outside and cited by link and SHA-256.
- **`github-separation`:** a change to `.github/` stands alone in its own pull request.
- **The signature-floor check** carries a reading: the floor guards 49GAION's own Standard and every edition at the handover; after the handover an organisation keeps its own floor list by its own rules, within the law (DR-0042, item 1).
