# 49GAION Standard v2.1.1

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decisions: [`DR-0051`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0051-hoac-assignment-model-in-the-top-management-group.md) and [`DR-0052`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0052-two-reviews-of-v2-1-0-corrected.md). Two reviews of `v2.1.0` were checked against `main` and corrected in one round, on the governing seat's word of 29 October 2026 (translated from Persian): "build it all at once; combine both and go through one correction round."

## Summary

### The HOAC Assignment Model (DR-0051)

- How an organisation assigns its HOAC positions to people is its HOAC Assignment Model: a feature of its Top Management Group, defined, recorded and kept only there, and set by each organisation. No other AIOC, no AI and no part of 49GAION reads it. One person holding every position is a model 49GAION introduces and promotes; the positions stay separate, and no single HOAC position covers everything.
- The withdrawn names (the AION Builder, its stages, Ionize, the delivery test) are no longer listed in the complete definition, the Standard's glossary or the Naming Standard; DR-0032 stays in force.

### Two decisions of the governing seat on the reviews (DR-0052)

- **Overseen.** An irreversible action under the Overseen link (a sent message, a payment, a deletion) runs only on the overseeing position's clearance; a lapsed hold window never releases it, and the lapse is raised. A reversible action keeps the lapse rule.
- **The Chief of Staff.** Each human position sets up its own Chief of Staff and may grant it up to the right to sign for it; within that grant it signs, tagged as a delegated act. The types of the Human Floor signature list stay with the position's own human signature.

### Corrections to the complete definition

- **The Standard:** the seat hands the operation to Top Management by a mandate (the bootstrap had kept "GOV appoints `[ORG]HOAC-TM`"); a label is held by one position; customer ownership names the Library and the central repositories; the naming example `[ORG]CELL-COSSL`; the gateway and the stop run as code beside the core, outside the reach of every AI.
- **The complete definition** says that each human position sets up its own Chief of Staff and may grant it up to the right to sign (§16), as the governing seat asked.
- **The Guide, Version 2,** is corrected in four phrases and says the same of the Chief of Staff; its PDF is published again.
- **The entry page** links the Guide, Version 2; the complete definition's test line records the run of 28 October 2026; the unit register form no longer points to the holders register; the decision index reads the records DR-0049 superseded in part.
- The wording of the rules that was superseded is listed at the end of each text, verbatim, under `v2.1.1`; corrections of a link, a label or a version are not listed.

### The checks
