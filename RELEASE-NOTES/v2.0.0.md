# 49GAION Standard v2.0.0

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decisions: four records since `v1.8.0`, [`DR-0044`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0044-three-mothers-in-repositories-of-49gaion.md) to [`DR-0047`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0047-the-clean-version.md), each listed with its subject in [`decisions/README.md`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/README.md). Released on the governing seat's words of 26 October 2026 (translated): "my confirmation was for all your proposals", the clean version among them.

## Summary

### The clean version (DR-0047)

- **What changed in the texts.** Since `v1.4.0` every change was additive: the old sentence stayed and a labelled Addition or Reading stood beside it; superseded wording was kept with a label. At `v2.0.0` each text states its current meaning in its body: every Addition is folded in, every Reading has replaced the sentence it read, and the wording that was superseded is listed once, verbatim, at the end of each text under "Superseded wording, by version". Each section that folded anything ends with a line naming the records it drew on.
- **What did not change.** No rule. No heading number, rule id, Human Floor item, defined term or code, with one exception: Standard §1.2 is now "The seven parts of 49GAION", the 49GAION Blueprint among them since DR-0041.
- **One source each.** The complete definition (`49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md`) is the one text of what 49GAION is; the Standard is the one text of its rules; `49GAION.md` is the short entry to both, its eight sections kept by name; the README is navigation. The Guide, Version 1, is unchanged.
- **From now on** each release is written clean, and the wording a release supersedes is kept at the previous tag and listed at the end of the text.
- **The `no-removal` baseline** of `v1.4.0` guarded the texts through this release; after the merge a `.github` change generates the baseline from the clean texts and makes it the default.
- **A major version,** because the wording of every text moves and the baseline moves with it; the rules are those of `v1.8.0`.
- **Checked.** Each clean text was compared with its layered original by a reader who did not write it, for any rule lost, changed or added; the findings were fixed before this release. The cold-read test was run again on the clean entry texts, answers verbatim: `references/2026-10-26-test-cold-read-v2-raw.md`.

### The mothers in repositories of 49GAION (DR-0044)

- The mother blueprints of Target Operating Model, Process and Position Design and Delivery and Implementation are in repositories of 49GAION, `49GAION-<part>`, like the other Library parts; the Library register pins them there. The instance repositories they came from are archived with a pointer.

### The owner and the licence (DR-0045, DR-0046)

- `LICENSE.md` in every public repository names the copyright holder; the texts are under CC BY 4.0 with the credit "Powered by 49GAION", the code under Apache-2.0, the names and marks not licensed. The full licence texts are in `LICENSE` and `LICENSES/`. The governing seat confirmed the licence in its own word (DR-0046).

### The structure review of 26 October 2026

- The hub carries `SECURITY.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`; `AGENTS.md` points to the complete definition first; the cold-read test was run again on the current entry texts with the answers kept verbatim (`references/2026-10-26-test-cold-read-raw.md`); the Standard's §4.2 item 10 and the signature list's rule state DR-0042's readings directly; the mother repositories pin the Protocol, carry `CODEOWNERS` and a `no-binaries` check.
