# The cold-read test of the complete definition · 25 October 2026

The rule the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION set on 25 October 2026 for the complete definition (`49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md`, DR-0041) and for the repositories of 49GAION: the writer and every reviewer read the text cold, and must be able to answer a fixed set of questions from it alone. If they can, the text is complete.

## The rule

1. **Cold.** Each reader is a separate engine with no context: no conversation, no earlier drafts, no notes. It gets only the text under test and the questions.
2. **Only the text.** Every answer comes from the text, citing the parts it rests on. Where the text gives no answer, the reader writes NOT IN THE TEXT and says what is missing. A matter the text leaves on purpose to the organisation's AI at the time is not a gap. Commercial terms are outside the definition.
3. **Separately,** in one round.
4. **Readers:** at least three engines from different providers, one of them a fresh instance of the engine that wrote the text.
5. **What is tested:** the definition first; then the repositories, read together.

## The questions

1. What is 49GAION, in two sentences, and what exactly does an organisation receive from it?
2. What makes it special: what does it do that a conventional way of designing an organisation, or of adding AI to one, does not?
3. How does an organisation start with it: step by step, from the first request to a running operation?
4. How is it carried out in practice: which parts must exist, what does each do, and who builds them?
5. Who decides what: the governance seat, Top Management, the organisation's `[ORG]AION`, and the AI units? What can AI never do?
6. How does a human stay in control and accountable?
7. How does it stay current as AI improves, and how does the organisation grow or shrink over time?
8. What does the organisation own, what stays with 49GAION, and when is a product "Powered by 49GAION"?
9. Does the same framework fit one person with one task and a group of companies? How?
10. Is it executable? The three strongest reasons it can work and the three biggest risks, and whether the text answers each risk or leaves it to the organisation's AI on purpose.
11. What would a new organisation ask that the text cannot answer?

## Passing

Every reader answers questions 1 to 9 from the text; the readers agree in substance; every risk named is answered or left to AI on purpose; nothing listed under question 11 belongs in the definition by the governing seat's decisions. A gap found is fixed in the text and the test is run again.
