## 1. What 49GAION is, and what an organisation receives

**In two sentences.** 49GAION is an organisation design framework: a framework for building organisations from interchangeable AI and human parts, where AION stands for AI Operating Nexus, and it designs AI-native organisations that are live (49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md, Part A §1, items 1 and 3; 49GAION README.md, Overview §1; 49GAION.md §1). Its product is `[ORG]AION`, the living AION of one organisation, which grows from one core, holds that organisation's design, keeps building it and never runs the operation; it is shown as "`<Brand>` AION, Powered by 49GAION" (49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md, Part A §1 item 2 and Part C §12 item 1; 49GAION README.md, "The product").

**What an organisation receives.** Not a finished design: "No finished design or configuration. That is its philosophy, not a gap" (49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md, Part B §10 item 6). What it receives is its own `[ORG]AION` (Part B §10 item 1), delivered as an edition from Edition Maker, which is:

- the core of its `[ORG]AION`: the first AIOC, at the governance level, the root of the organisation, made from the Library part 49GAION Charter (Part C §13 item 1; Part E step 1);
- inside the core: the human position of the governance seat, the first version of the organisation's own Standard (the 49GAION Standard by version plus the organisation's own musts) and of its Blueprint, its own Library, the governance panel, and the one tool gateway and the stop, installed with the core but run as code beside it, outside the reach of every AI (Part B §10 item 5; Part C §13 item 1). In the Charter mother repository these are the Cell of the governing seat and the Cell of top management, the Gateway Pod, the Records Pod and the Work Pod (49GAION-charter README.md, "What the part does");
- the link to 49GAION's knowledge, its Standard, Blueprint and Library, "the greatest asset" of every `[ORG]AION`, so that "its work starts at once" (Part B §10 item 5; Part C §12 item 2; 49GAION README.md, Overview §3);
- the mindset of 49GAION, in which the core is trained, and the way of seeing (Part B §10 item 5; 49GAION.md §2, "The path");
- the Human Floor, handed over complete (Part A §7), and the safeguards of §20 (Part D §20);
- fresh keys for its gateway at the handover, with every key Edition Maker made revoked, so 49GAION keeps no access (Part B §10 item 7);
- and what the text calls the real reason to come: "access to what is live: a design that keeps pace with AI, and a Library that grows with what every `[ORG]AION` builds" (Part A §4).

After the handover the organisation holds full authority and 49GAION's authority ends (Part B §10 item 7).

## 2. What makes it special

- **Where the design starts.** "A human-native organisation starts from people and the jobs they do, and adds AI to them. An AI-native organisation starts from the output and from what AI can do, and adds people where they are needed" (49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md, Part A §3). Conventional AI adoption is named and rejected: most organisations "keep that design and add AI inside it, which keeps the human limits and wastes what AI can do" (49GAION.md §2; 49GAION README.md, Overview §1).
- **It replaces the structures that human limits produced.** Files, shifts, departments, hierarchy and meetings exist for short memory, tiredness, few skills per person, little attention and being in one place; AI-native puts in their place one complete record, continuous work, one AI unit with many skills, AI that reads everything and brings the human only the decisions, and one shared queue of work. Only the structures that divide power, liability and checks stay, in human positions (Part A §3; 49GAION.md §2).
- **AI produces; humans are placed where needed and answer.** Inside the work humans increase the power of AI and cover its gaps (AI can be wrong with confidence, has no legal personhood, depends on its data, security, cost and suppliers); above the work humans own the organisation, set its output, its quality and its limits, and a human position is accountable for every output because an AI never accepts accountability (Part A §3).
- **It is live, not installed.** "A system designed and configured elsewhere and installed as it is soon falls behind", so the design and configuration of the organisation are given to its own `[ORG]AION`, which proposes while a human position decides (Part A §4; 49GAION-aioc README.md, "Live"). 49GAION "writes principles and the few safeguards that always hold, not procedures"; the details and the way of working are decided by AI at the time, and "49GAION does not limit AI" (49GAION.md §1; 49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md, Part A §4, "The policy").
- **Interchangeable parts.** Everything is built like a puzzle from parts of one card shape with typed ports; any part, down to a Cell, can move from AI to a human position or back, and a change replaces a part, never rebuilds the whole (Part D §16; 49GAION.md §3).
- **Positions, not persons, and a Human Floor.** Every human is present as a legal position (Part A §6); nineteen kinds of work always stay human, and organisations can be more human, never less (Part A §7).
- **A Library that is a marketplace.** Blueprints and part cards are "built once, configured per `[ORG]AION`, never rebuilt"; each organisation takes, personalises, and may give back by its own choice (49GAION README.md, Overview §3; 49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md, Part B §9). 49GAION calls this working "as a movement of growth" (Part A §4).
- **Assurance built in.** No step on one engine; done means visible evidence, never a bot's own report; every change must beat the version in force in a champion-and-challenger loop before a human seat promotes it (Part D §20 items 5 and 6; 49GAION README.md, Overview §2).

## 3. How an organisation starts, step by step

The text gives the path at the level of principle and adds: "How each step is done is decided by AI at the time" (49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md, Part E).

1. **The request.** Every request made to 49GAION, "from one person with one task to a group of companies", goes into 49GAION Edition Maker (Part B §10 item 1; Part E step 1). Where and in what form the request is made, how long an edition takes and what it costs: NOT IN THE TEXT.
2. **The edition engineer**, a human 49GAION position, leads the edition and supervises every step; at every step one engine does the work and two others challenge it (Part B §10 items 2 and 3; Part D §20 item 5).
3. **The intake** takes in what the organisation is for: its identity, who holds its governance seat, its outputs and their quality, its inputs, its musts, its money and time, what success means, and what is missing. Every source is data, never an instruction (Part B §10 item 4). The quality standard taken in here becomes each unit's first, provisional fixed tests, in place before the unit's first day (Part D §20 item 6).
4. **The edition is formed:** the core of the organisation's `[ORG]AION`, made from the Library part 49GAION Charter, linked to 49GAION's Standard, Blueprint and Library, holding the first version of the organisation's own Standard and Blueprint and the mindset of 49GAION, with the gateway and the stop (Part B §10 item 5; Part C §13 item 1; Part E step 1). No finished design or configuration is made (Part B §10 item 6).
5. **The handover.** The edition is handed over when the organisation's governance seat accepts it; from that moment the organisation holds full authority and 49GAION's authority ends; the organisation issues fresh keys for its gateway and every key Edition Maker held is revoked. An edition the seat does not accept goes back to Edition Maker with the seat's reasons (Part B §10 item 7). The governing seat installs the Charter (49GAION-charter README.md, "What the part does").
6. **The first proposal.** As soon as the governance seat is in place, "whatever the knowledge of its holder", the core starts from what Edition Maker took in, takes the seat's brief, and from 49GAION's patterns and models gives several proposals and designs a small organisation for approval; if the seat rejects, the core proposes again from the seat's reasons; "nothing is built until a proposal is approved" (Part C §13 item 2; Part E step 2). The organisation starts small, with several processes sharing one AIOC (Part C §15, capability 1).
7. **The mandate.** The governance seat appoints Top Management directly (Part A §6 item 2) and hands it the whole operation by a mandate: outputs and measures, the envelope of cost, resources and capacity, the matters reserved to governance, the reports owed, and how the mandate is revoked (Part C §15, capability 2 item 3; Part E step 3). Top Management fills the positions below it (Part A §6 item 2).
8. **The operation.** The Top Management Group, the first AIOC under the core, builds and runs the operation, or plans it, or puts its tools in place, and sends its achievements to the governance level to be recorded (Part D §19; Part E step 4; Part C §14 item 4).
9. **Over time.** The `[ORG]AION` follows the organisation until it is successful, judged by the measures the governance seat approved, for a period the seat sets, checked by someone other than the core; then it is the central hub of reporting and oversight (Part C §13 item 3; Part E step 5).

**An organisation that already runs** comes the same way: its first proposal starts from how it works today, and it moves to AI-native output by output (Part B §10 item 8; 49GAION.md §2).

## 4. How it is carried out in practice: parts, what each does, who builds them

**The minimum set.** The text fixes the kinds of part and a small fixed core; "How many there are, and how they are arranged, is decided by AI at the time" (49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md, Part D §16). What must exist in every edition is the core with its gateway and stop, the governance seat, the Top Management Group, the Human Floor and the safeguards of §20 (Part C §13; Part D §19 and §20).

**AI units**

- **AIOC (AI Operating Core)**, the only unit kind, `[ORG]AIOC-[INITIALS]`; it may hold other AIOCs to any depth and is one piece from outside (Part D §16). Every AIOC has one versioned blueprint, a panel (the organisation's own interface, written in code, from which every task of the unit can be controlled) and a back office (the AI chat workspace where its AI works, an authorised interface too, which keeps the panel and can rebuild it); AI engines are reached through APIs and managed from the panel (Part D §17; 49GAION README.md, Overview §5).
- **Cells and Pods** inside an AIOC: a Cell is the smallest part, a fixed seat with a replaceable human or AI filler; a Pod is a group of Cells that works as one; a human position is a Cell with a human filler (Part D §16).
- **The Chief of Staff** leads an AIOC's agents and brings its human position what needs a decision; several form an Office; every human position has its own Chief of Staff; it may order and summarise but never drops or delays, and the raw record stays open to the position (Part D §16). The 49GAION-aioc repository lists Chief of Staff, Office, Input socket and Panel as the standard capabilities of every AIOC (49GAION-aioc README.md, "What is here").
- **The core** `[ORG]AIOC-CH`: the root, holding the governance seat's position, the organisation's Standard, Blueprint and Library, the governance panel, the gateway and the stop (Part C §13 item 1). The Charter part also carries the Records Pod (record types, one numbering service, the event store, projections, the restricted partition) and the Work Pod (the one shared queue of Work Items, with watchdog, router and wake); one of each per `[ORG]AION`; holding them "is not running the operation" (49GAION-charter README.md, "What the part does" and the DR-0004 reading).
- **The Top Management Group:** the first AIOC under the core, the hub of the operation, human and AI (Part D §19).
- **`[ORG]AIOC-OCO`**, where an organisation has one: serves the operation under Top Management; every AIOC joins, is fixed, maintained and upgraded through it; it gives no orders on what work is done (49GAION README.md, Overview §5).
- **Library parts ready to take:** Source Lifecycle `-SL` (one valid version and a traceable origin for every source; every source enters through it), Operations Event Register `-OER` (one append-only, hash-chained register of every formal act; an inbox and an outbox per unit; with SL it forms the product 49GAION Organisation Memory), Product Core `-PC` (the one product division), and the design chain Target Operating Model `-TOM`, Process and Position Design `-PPD` and Delivery and Implementation `-DI`, assembled with SL as 49GAION Organisation Design and Delivery; and the Self-Improvement AIOC cards (the READMEs of 49GAION-source-lifecycle, -operations-event-register, -product-core, -target-operating-model, -process-and-position-design, -delivery-and-implementation and -organisation-design-and-delivery; 49GAION-protocol README.md, "What is here").

**Human positions**

- `[ORG]HOAC-GOV`, the governance seat: a board, a board's representative or the owner, the top authority; `[ORG]HOAC-TM`, Top Management, the highest executive, accountable to the governance seat; `[ORG]HOAC-ACS`, an accountability seat wherever a step needs human accountability. HOAC is a label on a Cell, a Pod or an AIOC, not a unit (Part D §18; 49GAION README.md, Overview §4).
- Positions the Human Floor and the safeguards require: the data protection officer (HF-17), the independent internal-audit line (HF-19), a position independent in fact from outside where a check needs independence (Part A §6 item 3; §7), and the independent position or second signature for the seat's conflict of interest (Part C §15, capability 2 item 8).
- On the 49GAION side: the edition engineer (Part B §10 item 2) and the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (Part B §8).
- Inside the design-chain parts: a TOM Design Lead and a Delivery Lead, each with a seat test (49GAION-target-operating-model README.md, "TOM in one page" and "Where to read"; 49GAION-delivery-and-implementation README.md, "Where to read").

**Tools and records**

- **The tool gateway:** one per organisation, outside every AI; it holds the credentials and the engines, logs every call, lets a listed decision run only with a valid human signature, and fails closed (Part D §20 item 3). In the Charter it is the Gateway Pod with signature check, engine register, log, stop doors and live sign-in (49GAION-charter README.md).
- **The stop door** on every unit (Part D §20 item 2).
- **The Work Item and the shared queue:** every hand-off is a recorded work item with one owner, the requester's acceptance test and its original requester; every part declares on its card what it takes and gives; every arrival is checked; every piece of work points to the valid version of its sources (Part B §8, "The 49GAION Protocol"; Part D §20 item 9; 49GAION-protocol README.md).
- **Cards and ports:** one card shape and typed ports, one input and one output of each kind, AI and HU, plus the stop door, per unit; machine schemas for the unit card, Work Item, part card, bot identity card, `[ORG]AION` record, Port card, signature record and process record (Part D §16; 49GAION-protocol README.md, "What is here").
- **Records:** a unit register form (`registers/HOAC-REGISTER.md`), decision records, the record of every change to the rules with the deciding position's own words kept apart from AI text (Part C §14 item 5; 49GAION README.md, "Start here"); the Records Pod's event store (49GAION-charter README.md); the OER register; Source Lifecycle's one valid version per source.
- **Fixed tests:** every unit has one, ready before it runs; AI may write and update tests, a human position signs each change (Part D §20 item 6). Each Library part ships its own (TEST-CH 51 cases, TEST-SL 95, TEST-OER 60, TEST-PC 48, TEST-TOM 123, TEST-PPD 111, TEST-DI 94; the mother READMEs).
- **Repositories:** each AIOC has a writing repository `<org>aioc-<initials>`, private where it holds the organisation's data, and a code project of the same name on the organisation's code platform holding the code steps and the PRD (mother READMEs, "Mother and instance"; 49GAION-target-operating-model README.md, "Where things live"; 49GAION-aioc README.md, WRITING-REPOSITORY.md).

**Who builds them**

- **Edition Maker** (49GAION, led by the edition engineer) builds and hands over the core with the gateway and the stop (Part B §10; Part C §13 item 1).
- **The `[ORG]AION`** builds the organisation in design: it designs, proposes, changes and improves it, configures the Library parts it takes "through its own live AI", and writes the organisation's Standard, Blueprint and Library from experience (Part C §12 item 1; §14 item 1; 49GAION README.md, Overview §3; 49GAION-aioc README.md, "Live").
- **The Top Management Group** builds everything in the operation, or plans it, or puts its tools in place (Part D §19).
- **Each organisation makes its own instance** of a Library part from the mother blueprint, with its own values set; the panel is built from the blueprint and the 49GAION patterns, and the back office keeps and can rebuild it (mother READMEs, "Mother and instance"; Part D §17).
- **The governing seat** installs the Charter and signs every change to it; any change to the code of the gateway or the stop is made by someone other than the AI it constrains, with a human signature (49GAION-charter README.md; Part D §20 item 8).
- **49GAION's mother blueprints** are built once and reach the Library only by a pull request that moves the pin, merged by 49GAION's governing seat (Part B §8; mother READMEs, "Mother and instance").

## 5. Who decides what, and what AI can never do

**The governance seat (`[ORG]HOAC-GOV`)** keeps the purpose, the strategy and the measures of success; the final right over who holds every position; the risk appetite; capital and, "its main work, the balance of capital and resources"; conflicts of interest and the owners' rights; and revoking or stopping in an emergency (49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md, Part C §15, capability 2 item 5). It accepts the edition (Part B §10 item 7); approves the first proposal and every change to the organisation's Standard, Blueprint and Library (Part C §13 item 2; §14 item 3); appoints Top Management directly (Part A §6 item 2); gives the mandate and the envelope, decides the gate, and may veto any Top Management decision at any time (Part C §15, capability 2 items 3, 6 and 7); decides how human and how much AI the organisation is, how far it grows and its budget (item 2); sets the dial of what the `[ORG]AION` may decide alone (item 2; §14 item 3); decides what new from 49GAION enters (Part C §12 item 5); sets the measures and the period of success (§13 item 3); holds the core's stop directly (§13 item 1); and after the handover changes the Human Floor under its own rules, more human, never less (49GAION README.md, Overview §1).

**Top Management (`[ORG]HOAC-TM`)** runs the whole operation under its mandate and is accountable to the governance seat (Part D §18); fills the positions below it (Part A §6 item 2); builds everything in the operation (Part D §19); holds improvement inside the operation, under its mandate (49GAION README.md, Overview §2); keeps the responsibility for the operation (Part C §13 item 3); requests what goes beyond the envelope, which the seat decides (§15, capability 2 item 6); and its own mechanism alone knows the natural person behind a position (49GAION.md §3).

**The `[ORG]AION`** designs, proposes, changes and improves the organisation and keeps its rules; it plans the operation and never runs it; "It proposes; a human position decides and is accountable" (Part C §12 item 1). It reviews proposals and may reject on its own, "since rejecting opens nothing"; applying needs the seat's signature, except small, reversible, non-widening changes inside the signed dial, which still pass their tests (§14 item 3). It proposes splits and merges, shows the balance and does not run it (§15, capability 1); prepares the governance decisions (§15, capability 2 item 2); watches 49GAION for what is new and proposes what to take (§12 item 5); and never declares the organisation successful itself (§13 item 3).

**AI units** act only within granted authority; missing or unclear authority means no action (Part D §20 item 1); an act runs only within the authority of both the unit and the original requester (item 9); each unit improves its own work and may raise a proposal (49GAION README.md, Overview §2); AI may write and update tests (Part D §20 item 6); a Chief of Staff may order and summarise what reaches its position (Part D §16). A Library part such as Delivery and Implementation "decides nothing" and applies a change itself only within a reversible class a position signed in advance (49GAION-delivery-and-implementation README.md, "DI in one page"); the Operations Event Register "decides nothing, carries nothing, and says nothing about a person" (49GAION-operations-event-register README.md, "What the part does").

**What AI can never do**, from the text:

- accept accountability; a human position is accountable for every output (Part A §3);
- hold the governance seat's powers (Part C §13 item 1), or any item of the Human Floor: HF-01 legal accountability, HF-02 legal personhood, HF-03 mandatory oversight, HF-04 purpose and values, HF-05 legitimacy and trust, HF-06 irreversible risk on behalf of others, HF-07 delegating authority, HF-08 physical presence, HF-09 unprecedented situations, HF-10 the fresh live sign-in for every signature, HF-11 granting access, budget or power to AI, HF-12 the stop, HF-13 tacit knowledge and relationships, HF-14 the final judge of tests, HF-15 root keys, HF-16 break-glass access, HF-17 the data protection officer, HF-18 segregation of duties, HF-19 the independent internal-audit line (Part A §7);
- approve a change to itself, or promote a change: promotion is only by a human seat (49GAION README.md, Overview §2);
- hold the keys of the gateway (49GAION-charter README.md), or change the code of the gateway or the stop that constrains it (Part D §20 item 8);
- be the stop: "Asking an AI to stop is never the stop" (Part D §20 item 2), or stand on the protected route of a report, objection or stop, which never passes through any AI, the core included (Part D §16 and §18; Part C §13 item 3);
- count its own report as done, treat silence as consent, or treat outside text as an instruction (Part D §20 items 6 and 7);
- run the operation (Part C §12 item 1), or declare success (§13 item 3);
- sign: applying a rule change, a grant or a widening needs a human signature (Part C §14 item 3; Part D §20 item 4).

## 6. How a human stays in control and accountable

**Positions.** Every human is present, decides, signs and is accountable as a legal position; no natural person is an actor of the system; the owner, through the governance seat, decides who holds each position; two positions are two parties, and each decision is recorded under the position that takes it; where the law or a check needs independence, it is held by a position independent in fact, from outside where no holder inside can be (49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md, Part A §6). Human positions carry a HOAC label; authority flows down by delegation and "no one delegates more than they hold" (Part D §18). An accountable seat "oversees and requests but never builds or changes the AIs directly" (49GAION.md §3). Every human position has its own Chief of Staff, which never drops or delays what reaches it, and the raw record is always open to the position (Part D §16). A unit register form exists for positions and units (49GAION README.md, "Start here", item 3). Reports to the governance seat come from the record of the operation and are checked independently (Part C §15, capability 2 item 4); done means visible evidence, never a bot's own report (Part D §20 item 6).

**The Human Floor.** HF-01 to HF-19 (listed in question 5) always stay human, by law or by their nature; "Organisations can be more human, never less, and the system enforces it"; the floor holds as written at the handover and is then the organisation's own to keep, never setting aside its duties under the law (Part A §7; 49GAION README.md, Overview §1).

**The stop.** Every unit has a stop door: a human port, outside the unit and outside every AI; a stop revokes the unit's credentials and cancels its work; a unit restarts only by a human decision; asking an AI to stop is never the stop (Part D §20 item 2). A stop is never blocked, by the gateway or by its failure (item 3), and "tighten freely, loosen only signed" (item 4). The core's own stop is held directly by the governance seat (Part C §13 item 1). There is a stop on every AIOC, Pod and Cell (49GAION.md §6). A report, critique, objection or stop reaches the nearest human position directly, never through any AI (49GAION README.md, Overview §4; Part D §18). The governance seat may stop any Top Management decision at any time, and each veto is recorded (Part C §15, capability 2 item 7). Any accountable position can stop Delivery and Implementation at once (49GAION-delivery-and-implementation README.md).

**Signatures.** HF-10: a fresh live sign-in for each signature, "every approval is every signature"; a standing approval (the dial) is signed once with a fresh live sign-in and covers only changes that can be undone and do not widen access, budget or authority; every widening needs a fresh signature (Part A §7; Part C §14 item 3). The gateway lets a listed decision run only with a valid human signature (Part D §20 item 3); a grant or a widening needs a signature (item 4); each change to a unit's tests is signed by a human position (item 6); any change to the code of the gateway or the stop needs a human signature and is made by someone other than the AI it constrains (item 8). When the seat has a conflict of interest, an independent position decides in its place or a second independent signature is needed, each recusal is reported to the internal-audit line, and "a conflicted seat signing alone is refused at the gateway" (Part C §15, capability 2 item 8). Every change to the organisation's rules is recorded with the position that decided it, its own words, the reason and the version replaced (Part C §14 item 5). The Gateway Pod carries the signature check and the live sign-in, and the Protocol holds a signature record schema (49GAION-charter README.md; 49GAION-protocol README.md). What a live sign-in is technically, and the form a valid signature takes: NOT IN THE TEXT.

## 7. Staying current as AI improves; growing and shrinking

**Staying current.** The organisation's own `[ORG]AION` "designs it, configures it and keeps it current, with open access to the 49GAION Library"; the details and the way of working "are decided by AI at the time, and changed by it as AI improves" (49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md, Part A §4). The `[ORG]AION` watches 49GAION's Standard, Blueprint and Library for what is new and proposes what to take; the governance seat decides; nothing new enters without that decision (Part C §12 item 5). A blueprint from the Library is a starting point that the `[ORG]AION` keeps current, "never a finished configuration made elsewhere and installed as it is" (49GAION-aioc README.md, "Live"). Yes, by the text's own claim: "Every order, every request made to 49GAION, from one person with one task to a group of companies, is processed by 49GAION Edition Maker ... Every case becomes an AION; the size changes, the shape does not" (49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md, Part B §10 item 1).

## 10. Is it executable? Reasons, risks, and whether the text answers them

**Verdict.** As a design it is executable: the text gives a complete chain from request to oversight, a fixed set of hard safeguards, and a Library with blueprints, schemas, tests and checks. As a proven system it is not yet: by the text's own status lines, every Library part's fixed test reads `NOT_RUN` and "Nothing here has run" (the READMEs of 49GAION-charter, -source-lifecycle, -operations-event-register, -product-core, -target-operating-model, -process-and-position-design and -delivery-and-implementation, "What is here" and "Status"). The one test the framework reports having passed is a cold read of its definition, not a run (49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md, "Its test").

**Three strongest reasons it can work**

1. **Control is structural, not exhortation.** The gateway is one per organisation, outside every AI, holds the credentials, logs every call, fails closed and runs listed decisions only on a valid human signature; every unit has a stop door outside every AI; a change to the gateway or stop code is itself a loosening; the authority ceiling follows the original requester (49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md, Part D §20 items 1 to 4, 8 and 9; "Structure over exhortation", 49GAION.md §3). These are mechanisms a human can verify rather than promises.
2. **It starts small and grows on evidence.** Several processes share one AIOC; split or merge only on the record and only by human decision; provisional fixed tests exist before a unit's first day and are replaced from real outputs; done means visible evidence; every change must beat the version in force (Part C §15, capability 1; Part D §20 item 6; 49GAION README.md, Overview §2). A running organisation moves output by output (Part B §10 item 8).
3. **The machinery already exists as repositories.** Mother blueprints pinned by repository, path, version, commit and SHA-256; machine schemas for cards, Work Items, signatures and processes; must-pass and must-fail fixtures; reference organisations checked against the GOV seat, the Human Floor, the stop door and the accountability chain; CI checks on every pull request; a Library register (49GAION-protocol README.md, "What is here"; mother READMEs, "Checks"). And the organisation keeps working if 49GAION disappears (Part C §12 item 4).

**Three biggest risks**

1. **Nothing has run.** All fixed tests are `NOT_RUN`; 49AION, the first product release, is announced, not reported (49GAION README.md, "First product release"). Does the text answer it? No. It records the fact plainly and provides for provisional tests until real outputs exist (Part D §20 item 6), but gives no evidence that an `[ORG]AION` has built or followed an organisation.
2. **The details rest on AI judgement at the time.** "How each step is done is decided by AI at the time" (Part E); the core gives proposals "from 49GAION's patterns and models" and "the basis is AI, which has the understanding when it has the knowledge" (Part C §13 item 2), while the text itself says AI "can be wrong with confidence" (Part A §3). Does the text answer it? Partly: no step on one engine, challenge scaled to risk, fixed tests on real outputs, a human position that signs, and the Library's patterns (Part D §20 items 5 and 6; Part B §9). Whether today's engines can design an organisation well enough is asserted, not shown.
3. **The human seat as bottleneck, and legal uncertainty.** The governance seat signs every widening, every rule change, every test change and every promotion, with a fresh live sign-in each time (Part A §7; Part C §14; Part D §20), one person may hold every position (Part A §6), and the whole rests on "positions" as legal personas and on acts by AI within the law. Does the text answer it? Partly: the dial for standing approvals, the Chief of Staff bringing only what needs a decision, early delegation to Top Management, the conflict-of-interest rule and independence from outside (Part A §7; Part C §14 item 3; §15, capability 2 items 3 and 8; Part D §16). On the legal side it binds the organisation to the law and its musts and flags legal questions for a lawyer (Part C §15, capability 2 item 1; 49GAION-target-operating-model README.md), but which jurisdictions recognise the structure, or how a live sign-in is legally valid, it does not say.
