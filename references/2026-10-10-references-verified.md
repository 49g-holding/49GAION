## Summary of key corrections
- METR: ~7-month doubling is correct but for the 50%-success time horizon on software/ML benchmark tasks (2019-2025); 80%-success horizons are much shorter; possibly faster since 2024.
- EU AI Act Art. 14: requires high-risk systems be designed for effective human oversight, proportionate to risk; not human sign-off on every decision.
- Tetlock: superforecasters were selected, trained top amateurs; benchmark was intelligence analysts (~30%, GJP's own figure), not "famous experts".
- Condorcet: holds only under independence and p > 1/2 per voter; asymptotic result.
- Janis: about concurrence-seeking in cohesive groups, not "strongest voice"; independent-first collection is from Noise.
- Valve: single ex-employee anecdote.
- GDPR DPO: mandatory only in Art. 37(1) cases (or national law); contact details, not a name, must be published; can be external/shared.
- Segregation of duties: frameworks separate authorize/custody/record(/review), with alternative controls allowed.
- Three Lines: first and second lines are both management; only internal audit is independent; plus governing body and external assurance.
