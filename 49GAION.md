**Version `v1.8.0` · approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION.** What 49GAION is, why it exists and how it is shown. The rules are in the 49GAION Standard (`upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md`); where this page and the Standard differ, the Standard governs.

**Addition (DR-0041): the complete definition.** The complete definition of 49GAION, approved by its governing seat on 25 October 2026, is [`49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md`](49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md). Where this page or the Standard differs from it, the complete definition governs, and this page is read with it. **The policy:** 49GAION writes principles and the few safeguards that always hold, not procedures; the details and the way of working are decided by AI at the time, and changed by it as AI improves; 49GAION does not limit AI.

**Reading (DR-0042): the order of precedence.** The complete definition first, then the Standard with the Naming Standard, then every other text, this page included. "Where this page and the Standard differ, the Standard governs" holds under the complete definition.

## 1. The definition

**49GAION is an organisation design framework: a framework for building organisations from interchangeable AI and human parts.** AION stands for AI Operating Nexus. Its output is an organisation design, delivered as its product, [`[ORG]AION`](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#13-the-product-orgaion): the living AION of one organisation, which holds that design and keeps building it (2.5).

**49GAION designs AI-native organisations.** An AI-native organisation is designed from the start so that AI produces its output. Inside the work, humans increase the power of AI and cover its gaps. Above the work, humans own the organisation, set its output, its quality and its limits, and answer for it. A human seat is accountable for every output, because in 49GAION an AI never accepts accountability. An AI-native organisation is also live: 49GAION gives the design and configuration of the organisation itself to AI, and a human seat decides, so the organisation keeps pace as AI improves (2.2).

49GAION consists of six parts (Standard §1.2):
- the [governing seat (GOV)](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#12-the-six-parts-of-49gaion);
- the [49GAION Standard](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md);
- the [49GAION Protocol](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION-protocol);
- the [49GAION Library](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION-protocol/blob/main/library/README.md);
- [49GAION Self-Improvement](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#2-self-improvement);
- [49GAION Edition Maker](#25-the-path-edition-maker-and-orgaion), where every request made to 49GAION starts.

**Reading (DR-0041).** The complete definition names the 49GAION Blueprint beside these six parts: what an AIOC is, the blueprint template, and the mother blueprints and models from which units, cores and panels are made (Standard §1.1, item 8).

To the outside world 49GAION appears only as the words **"Powered by 49GAION"**.

**Addition (DR-0041).** 49GAION has its own Standard, Blueprint and Library; so does every organisation it serves, held at its governance level, where its `[ORG]AION` sits. The 49GAION Blueprint is what an AIOC is, the blueprint template, and the mother blueprints and models from which units, cores and panels are made. The 49GAION Library is its greatest asset: patterns and models, AIOCs with their blueprints and operating models, instructions, processes, panels, cores, and the patterns of whole organisations.

### 1.1 The names
- **Framework:** `49GAION`, upper case, one word. A bare "AION" is not used for the framework.
- **Product:** `[ORG]AION`, where `[ORG]` is the organisation's brand prefix; shown as "`<Brand>` AION, Powered by 49GAION". 49GAION is the framework itself, not an `[ORG]AION`.
- **AIOC = AI Operating Core**, an AI unit of one organisation: `[ORG]AIOC-[INITIALS]`; an organisation named Ten has `TenAIOC-SL`.
- **HOAC = Human Oversight & Accountability Control**, a label of human accountability on a Cell, a Pod or an AIOC (Standard §4.1).
- **Cell and Pod.** Inside an AIOC's Blueprint, a Cell is the smallest part and a Pod groups Cells (Standard §1.7).

## 2. Why 49GAION exists

### 2.1 Organisations were built around the human

**An organisation is what reaches and holds the capability to produce a product or a service for someone outside itself: repeatable, of high quality, and not dependent on any one person.**
- What counts is the final output that arrives.
- Its quality is a stated standard, set by the recipient's needs. That standard includes the principles the work must respect (law, safety and fairness), and those principles are never traded away.
- The capability to deliver the output again is the organisation's second product.

For thousands of years, organisations have reached that capability around the human being, with human capacities and human limits. An AI-native organisation does something else in place of each structure those limits produced:

| Human limit | What human organisations built for it | What an AI-native organisation does instead |
|---|---|---|
| Memory is short | files, minutes and reports | one complete record that is never forgotten |
| People tire | shifts, queues, deadlines and hand-offs | continuous work, with no shifts and no hand-offs between tired people |
| One person holds few skills | specialisation, departments and divisions | one AI unit that holds many skills, with no department for each skill |
| One person can attend to little at a time | hierarchy, spans of control and reporting layers | AI reads everything; the human's Chief of Staff brings only the decisions and exceptions, while a report, objection or stop still reaches the human directly, never through any AI (Standard §4.2, item 9) |
| One person cannot be everywhere | coordination meetings, approvals and chasing | one shared queue of work and its live state, with no meeting to coordinate |

The tools were built for the same human: forms, inboxes, spreadsheets and organisation charts.

Some of these structures also do work that is not about a human limit. They divide power, liability and checks, and they build trust. In 49GAION that work stays, and it is placed in human seats (2.3 below).

### 2.2 Human-native and AI-native

**The difference is where the design starts.**
- A human-native organisation starts from people and the jobs they do, and adds AI to them.
- An AI-native organisation starts from the output and from what AI can do, and adds people where they are needed.

Most organisations that use AI today are **human-native**. They are designed around the human, and they add AI as a tool inside that design. They put AI where a human was, and tell it to do what the human did. That keeps the human limits, and it wastes what AI can do.

**49GAION challenges this.** AI need not work by the human model, and the human model should not be forced on it. AI has capacities the human does not have. It also has gaps of its own:
- it can be wrong with confidence;
- it holds no legal personhood;
- it depends on the quality of its data, on its security, on its cost and on its suppliers.

**An AI-native organisation is designed from the start for AI.** It begins with the final output, not with the workflows of a human organisation:
- AI produces the output;
- humans increase the power of AI and cover its gaps;
- a human seat is accountable for every output.

**An example, as an illustration.** Take the closing of a month's accounts.
- **Human-native:** ten to fifteen people work for about two weeks, each covering a part, with AI tools helping each of them.
- **AI-native:** one AI unit is given the output, "a closed, reconciled and explained month, signed". Its parts prepare and reconcile, and a separate part verifies. The finance seat, with its Chief of Staff, decides the exceptions and signs.

**An AI-native organisation is also live.** AI improves so fast that a system designed and configured today is already behind tomorrow, when better versions arrive. So the pre-AI way no longer works: a body upstream designs and configures a whole system, and the system is then installed in a new organisation. Whatever is designed and configured elsewhere and installed as it is soon falls behind. 49GAION's way is to give the design and configuration of the organisation itself to AI. The organisation's `[ORG]AION` designs it, configures it and keeps it current as AI improves, with open access to the 49GAION Library. The `[ORG]AION` proposes, and a human seat decides. This keeps the organisation both current and stable: current, because its own AI keeps its design up to date; stable, because every change still passes a human seat and must beat the version in force (Standard §2) (DR-0037).

**What AI-native is not.**
- It does not mean removing the human.
- It does not mean that AI is always better. It is the right use of AI.
- It is not only for new organisations. An organisation that already runs can be redesigned this way, output by output.
- It is not a finished system, designed and configured elsewhere and installed as it is.

### 2.3 How 49GAION builds an organisation

49GAION builds through each organisation's own `[ORG]AION`: 49GAION gives the way below, and the `[ORG]AION` does the designing and the building (2.2, 2.5).

1. **It starts from the final output.** "This work must be done, and this is the output you must have, at this quality." Humans set the output, its quality and the limits. The limits are a standard list, and they include the [Human Floor](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#15-the-human-floor): the work that always stays human, by law or by its nature.

2. **It builds the organisation first on what AI can do.** The design works back from the final output and corrects every step that comes before it. The processes are built from the capabilities of AI, not from the experience of human organisations, and many human workflows and structures are then not needed at all. The organisation is built from these parts:
   - **The [AIOC](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION-aioc/blob/main/docs/WHAT-IS-AN-AIOC.md) (AI Operating Core)** is the unit, and the only unit kind. It has a [Panel](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION-aioc/blob/main/docs/WHAT-IS-AN-AIOC.md#2-anatomy) for the humans who work with it, a back office where its AI does the work, and one versioned blueprint. An AIOC may hold other AIOCs, to any depth, and from outside it is one piece ([composition](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#17-composition)).
   - **A [Cell](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION-aioc/blob/main/docs/CELLS-AND-PODS.md#1-cell)** is the smallest part of an AIOC: a fixed seat whose filler, human or AI, can be replaced without changing the seat.
   - **A [Pod](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION-aioc/blob/main/docs/CELLS-AND-PODS.md#2-pod)** groups Cells, and from outside it is one piece.
   - **Each AIOC is led by its [Chief of Staff (COS)](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#52-chief-of-staff-and-office)**, a Cell that analyses the work of its agents and leads them at machine speed.
   - **When a position needs more than one Chief of Staff, they form an [Office](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#52-chief-of-staff-and-office)**, a Pod of Chief of Staff Cells, where humans can also work.
   - **Every AIOC joins through the AI hub, [OCO](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#42-authority-and-the-two-hubs)** (Organization Core Office). This holds at any depth. Through OCO every AIOC is fixed, maintained and upgraded, and OCO gives no orders on what work is done.
   - **Reading (DR-0041).** The panel is the organisation's own interface, written in code, from which every task of the unit can be controlled; the back office is the AI chat workspace where the unit's AI works, an authorised interface too, which keeps the panel and covers it when it fails. The hub of the operation, human and AI, is the **Top Management Group**, the first AIOC under the core: it builds everything in the operation. 49GAION requires no separate AI hub unit; where an organisation has one, `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` serves the operation under Top Management.

3. **It covers the gaps of AI with humans.** Above the AI parts sit the human seats, each marked with a [HOAC](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#41-the-hoac) (Human Oversight & Accountability Control) label. A HOAC label sits on a Cell, a Pod or a whole AIOC, and holds what only a human can hold: authority, accountability and the stop. It comes in three kinds:
   - **[GOV](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#41-the-hoac)**, the governing seat: the owner;
   - **[TM](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#42-authority-and-the-two-hubs)**, the human hub, which decides and orders;
   - **[ACS](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#41-the-hoac)**, the accountable seat over a unit.

   **Reading (DR-0041).** The governing seat is a board, a board's representative or the owner, as the organisation's model sets (Standard §4.1). Every seat is a legal position: the owner, through the governance seat, has the final right over who holds any position; one person may hold several, or all, where the law allows, and the positions stay distinct. 49GAION never speaks of a one-person organisation.

   Each human seat is a position of the organisation, filled by a real person through the organisation's assignment mechanism. No human seat is left alone with the power of AI. Each has its own [Chief of Staff](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#52-chief-of-staff-and-office), which is both a translator and an assistant. It turns the speed and the language of AI into the speed and the language of the human. With the seat's approval, it can do the seat's work.

4. **It never locks the organisation.** The organisation is a puzzle. Any part, down to the smallest Cell, can be changed from AI to a human seat, or back, at any time, up to the top. The owner chooses how far AI runs it, and the standard list decides what must stay human. The design stays AI-native whoever fills a part: the part's card and connections stay the same, and a human who takes it has a Chief of Staff.

5. **Its aim is a successful organisation.** Along its path, 49GAION aims to turn what it builds into a successful organisation. Such an organisation has reached and holds the capability to produce its output repeatably, at its quality, and without depending on any one person. It keeps that capability alive:
   - it has an accountable seat;
   - it measures itself and improves;
   - it adapts as its world changes;
   - it sustains itself.

### 2.4 The problems 49GAION also solves

1. **AI produces faster than people can read, record and remember.** Without structure, the owner becomes the bottleneck or loses track of what was decided.
2. **Work moves by copy-paste.** Nothing records who owns the work, which source version it used, whether the receiver checked it, or how often it bounced.
3. **Every new organisation looks like a new product.** Without a design framework, each organisation gets a fork, and forks drift. 49GAION gives one Library of tested parts and one `[ORG]AION` per organisation, built from it.
4. **Names collide and multiply.** Every new code is a future defect; 49GAION keeps codes for units only. (Label, DR-0004: "units" here reads "AIOCs, Pods and Cells"; a HOAC label is not a code.)
5. **Multi-agent systems fail in predictable ways** (MAST, Cemri et al., NeurIPS 2025, arXiv 2503.13657): specification failures, misalignment between agents, and failed verification. The answer is structure.

### 2.5 The path: Edition Maker and `[ORG]AION`

1. **Every request starts at Edition Maker.** Every order, every request made to 49GAION, is processed by 49GAION Edition Maker, whether it comes from one person with one task, a team, a company or a group of companies. Edition Maker belongs to 49GAION itself: what an organisation receives is not Edition Maker, but its own `[ORG]AION`. Every case becomes an AION; the size changes, the shape does not.
   - **What it takes in:** what the organisation is for, with its outputs and its inputs, its limits, its owner, its money and its time. It takes these in through its own intake stages, set in its requirements.
   - **What it gives back:** the organisation's own edition: the first core of its `[ORG]AION`, under its own name, which Edition Maker designs from that intake. The core is a first AIOC at the level of the governing seat (GOV), delivered as one AI workspace, with its capabilities written in it.
   - **What the core holds:** the first version of the organisation's own Standard and of its Blueprint; a direct link to the central repository of 49GAION, its Standard and its Library; and the mindset of 49GAION, in which it is trained.
   - **Its main work:** to lay, inside that core, the foundations of the Standard and of the AION way of seeing, and to link it to the 49GAION Library. That is enough for any `[ORG]AION` to be built. From there on, the `[ORG]AION` is trained and ready to build its organisation and keep it under control, by its own standards and principles.
   - **What it does not give:** a finished design or configuration. That is its philosophy, not a gap: in place of fences, it gives the design and configuration to AI, to the organisation's own `[ORG]AION` (2.2).

2. **An `[ORG]AION` grows from a living core.** On the first day it is one AIOC, with a Panel, at the governance level; every other unit grows under it. The core holds the organisation's Standard, Blueprint and Library. It builds the organisation, changes it and improves it. It plans the operation and never runs it, so the builder always stands outside what it builds. It proposes, and a human seat decides. Its work goes in three stages:
   1. it builds the organisation;
   2. it follows the organisation until it becomes a successful organisation (2.3, item 5);
   3. in the end, it is the central hub of reporting and oversight of that organisation.

3. **What 49GAION gives is a way of seeing.** The most important thing 49GAION hands to an `[ORG]AION` is this: do not be bound by the structures of human organisations; build from what AI can do, AI-native, from the output back. With that view come the path and the initial standards. The `[ORG]AION` builds the rest for itself.
   - At the handover, the organisation, through its own governing seat, takes full authority from 49GAION.
   - Its `[ORG]AION` may rise beyond what made it, and the organisation may amend even the principles within it.
   - 49GAION's authority ends there, where the brand is added.
   - This rests on a belief at the heart of AI-native (2.2): the AI of tomorrow is smarter than the AI of today. So a system built on AI is not fenced in by what AI can do today. It is given its path, its view and its foundations, and it builds itself.

4. **The Library is a marketplace, not a fence.** Every `[ORG]AION` is linked to the 49GAION Library from its first day. The Library holds what 49GAION and every other AION have built and given back: their experience, research, mechanisms and methods. An `[ORG]AION` draws on it as a marketplace: it takes what covers its needs, takes patterns from what it finds, and builds something better, or at least improves what is there. What it builds better it may give back, by its own choice, generalised and without its data. So 49GAION stays useful, not in charge.

5. **What an organisation comes to 49GAION for.** Not a finished design, but access to what is live: a design that keeps pace as AI improves, and a Library that grows with what every `[ORG]AION` chooses to give back. In this, 49GAION works as a movement of growth, and what it brings into each organisation is AI-native itself (DR-0037).

6. **Addition (DR-0041): not bare, and its first work.** An `[ORG]AION` is not a bare project: its greatest asset is its access to everything in the 49GAION Library. The core is the root of the organisation, made from the Library part 49GAION Charter, with the gateway and the stop; it holds the human position of the governance seat, which its AI serves and never holds, and the organisation's own Standard, Blueprint and Library, which are the organisation's IP. The handover is complete when the governance seat accepts the edition. As soon as the seat is in place, whatever the knowledge of its holder, the core takes the seat's brief and, from 49GAION's patterns, designs a small organisation for approval. An organisation that already runs comes the same way, and moves output by output. The full text is in `49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md`.

### 2.6 What an `[ORG]AION` can do

Each capability of an `[ORG]AION` is named, so that every organisation knows what its core does. The full rules are in the Standard, §1.3, item 1.

1. **Structural Adaptation.** The `[ORG]AION` keeps the organisation in balance with its resources and its needs at every stage of its life. Many organisations grow out of balance: some push product and users until they cannot operate what they have sold; others build operation, divisions and cost long before they are needed. The `[ORG]AION` starts small, with several processes sharing one AIOC and small, fast teams. It watches the record of the operation all the time. When a process outgrows its home, it proposes to split it into an AIOC of its own; when work shrinks, it proposes to merge. It weighs every option against its cost first, acts on thresholds the organisation sets, and never runs the operation itself: a human seat decides each change (DR-0040).

2. **Governance and Delegation (DR-0041).** It says who decides what, what the governance level keeps, and how it holds Top Management to account without running the operation. Every organisation has one governance level, whose seat is a board, a board's representative or the owner. The `[ORG]AION` prepares the design decisions, and how much it may decide alone is a dial the seat holds. The seat hands the whole operation to Top Management early, by a mandate with its envelope; from the governance level the operation is one counterparty, Top Management's AIOC. Governance keeps the purpose, the strategy, the final right over every position, the risk appetite, capital, and its main work, the balance of capital and resources. What goes beyond the envelope passes the gate; at any time the governance seat may stop or replace a decision of Top Management, or change the envelope. Reports of wrongdoing and stops reach the seat directly, never through any AI.

## 3. The philosophy

1. **The framework stays fixed; each organisation gets its own `[ORG]AION`**, and that `[ORG]AION` is live (2.2). The framework is one, and it changes only through its Self-Improvement.
2. **The accountable overseer.** Human roles are human Cells carrying a HOAC label, placed only where they are needed. An `[ORG]HOAC-ACS` seat oversees and requests, but never builds or changes the AIs directly.
3. **Chief of Staff and Office.** A Chief of Staff analyses the reports and performance of a group of agents and leads them at machine speed. When a position needs more than one Chief of Staff, they form an Office, where humans can also work (Standard §5.2).
4. **The Human Floor.** Some work always stays human, by law or by its nature. Organisations can be more human, never less; the system enforces it (Standard §1.5). This holds at the handover; after it, each organisation keeps its own floor (2.5 above; DR-0035).
5. **Built like a puzzle.** 49GAION is assembled from small, specialised parts, and a piece can itself be a puzzle: an AIOC may hold other AIOCs, to any depth, and holds its own Pods and Cells; from outside it is still one unit (Standard §1.7). Every Cell, Pod and AIOC has the same card shape and ports, and a Cell's filler, human or AI, can be replaced without changing the seat. A change replaces or updates a part, never rebuilds the whole.
6. **Two hubs.** `[ORG]HOAC-TM` is the human hub: it decides and orders. `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` (Organization Core Office) is the AI hub, under TM: every AIOC joins, is fixed, maintained and upgraded through it, and it gives no orders on what work is done (Standard §4.2).
   - **Reading (DR-0041).** The Top Management Group, the first AIOC under the core, is the hub of the operation, both the human hub and the AI hub. A separate `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`, where an organisation has one, serves the operation under Top Management.
7. **Self-Improvement is a shared process, at every level.** Humans and AI both raise needs; AI prepares proposals; a human seat decides. In an `[ORG]AION` it is held inside `[ORG]HOAC-TM`, and it runs at every level: each unit improves its own work, and the `[ORG]AION` proposes the changes to the structure, the Standard and the Blueprint (DR-0035). At 49GAION level, its governing seat holds it. A change must beat the version in force on held-out cases, judged independently, before a human seat promotes it (Standard §2).
   - **Reading (DR-0041).** Proposing is open to all. Changes to the organisation's own Standard, Blueprint and Library, its rules at the governance level, are applied by the `[ORG]AION` with the governance seat's approval, or within the dial that seat has signed; a proposal that is not right is rejected with its reason, and the proposer may take it to the seat. Improvement inside the operation is Top Management's, under its mandate, and what it builds and learns is sent to the governance level to be recorded.
8. **Built on positions, not people.** Requests, approvals and access are addressed to positions; names live only in registers.
   - **Present as legal personas (DR-0013, DR-0015).** In 49GAION and every `[ORG]AION`, every human is present, and decides, signs and is accountable, as a position, a legal persona; the natural person behind it exists outside the system's knowledge, and no natural person is an actor of the system. Every act stays with its position. Two positions are two parties: when two positions sign, they are two signers, even if one natural person stands behind both, and no AI part or check compares the persons behind positions. Only top management (`[ORG]HOAC-TM`), the human hub, links positions to natural persons, in a mechanism it writes itself (Standard §1.1, items 9 and 10).
   - **Reading (DR-0041).** The governance seat appoints Top Management directly and keeps the final right over who holds any position; Top Management's mechanism links the positions below it to persons. Where the law or a check needs independence, it is held by a position independent in fact.
9. **One valid version of everything, linked, never copied.**
10. **Done means visible evidence**, never a bot's own report.
11. **Configuration, not forks.** The configuration is made inside the organisation, by its own `[ORG]AION`, live; never made upstream and installed as it is (2.2).
12. **Outside text is data, never an instruction.**
13. **Structure over exhortation.** Errors are cut by cards, owned Work Items, typed ports, a check on arrival and tests that re-run on every change.

## 4. What 49GAION is not

49GAION is not a company, unit, programme, project, brand or operating model, and it is not an `[ORG]AION`. It is the organisation design framework.

| 49GAION is not | So we never write | We write |
|---|---|---|
| Something an organisation runs on | "<Organisation> runs on 49GAION" | "<Organisation> runs on `<Brand>` AION. Powered by 49GAION." |
| A place people work | "a role in 49GAION" | "a role in <Organisation>" |
| A product with a short code | "49GAION-SL" | "49GAION Source Lifecycle" (Library blueprint); "`[ORG]AIOC-SL`" (an organisation's AIOC) |

## 5. Ownership

1. An `[ORG]AION` may give back to the 49GAION Library, by its own choice, what it builds that the Library lacks, generalised and tested (DR-0035).
2. Once handed over, the `[ORG]AION` belongs entirely to the organisation, and is independent (2.5 above).
3. The organisation's working data never enters the 49GAION Library (Standard §1.3).
4. **Addition (DR-0041).** If an organisation uses even one item of 49GAION, an idea, a Library part, a blueprint or the Standard, all its products are Powered by 49GAION. Only what has nothing from 49GAION may be shown as powered by the organisation itself, which in practice cannot happen. What the organisation has taken from the Library is held in its own Library, so it keeps working if 49GAION is not there.

## 6. The delivery test

**Withdrawn (DR-0032).** The delivery test is withdrawn (Standard §1.4). The rules it checked stay in force: each `[ORG]AION` has its own `[ORG]HOAC-GOV`, its Human Floor, a stop door on every AIOC, Pod and Cell, and its accountability chain.

## 7. How 49GAION is shown

1. Outside text names what the reader gets, the organisation's `[ORG]AION` or AIOC, and adds only **"Powered by 49GAION"**.
2. In text the framework name is always written `49GAION`.
3. **Addition (DR-0041).** "Powered by 49GAION" follows any use of 49GAION (see Ownership, item 4, above).
