# 49GAION · the central hub

This repository holds the 49GAION Standard, the master definition, the Naming Standard, the unit register form and the decision records.

## Overview

### 1. What 49GAION is

**49GAION** is an organisation design framework: a framework for building organisations from interchangeable AI and human parts. AION stands for AI Operating Nexus.

**The product: `[ORG]AION`**, the AI Operating Nexus of one organisation; its one definition is in the Standard, [§1.3, item 1](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#13-the-product-orgaion).

**The Human Floor.** Every `[ORG]AION` stands on a Human Floor: the work that always stays human, by law or by its nature (HF-01 to HF-19). Organisations can be more human, never less; the system enforces it.

**First product release.** The first `[ORG]AION` to be released is 49AION, the product of 49G. It is built under 49GAION as its own product, separate from this Standard.

### 2. Self-Improvement

Self-Improvement is a process shared by AI and humans. Any human seat or AI unit may raise a proposal; a proposal carries the current text, the proposed text, the reason, the evidence and the parts and tests it affects; it is discussed, and the discussion decides nothing; it takes effect only on approval by the accountable human seat. No unit approves a change to itself. In an `[ORG]AION` it is held inside `[ORG]HOAC-TM` and carried out with an AIOC within TM; at 49GAION level it is held by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (Standard §2). Every change is found and tested in a champion-and-challenger loop: a challenger is judged in pairs against the version in force, must win on a held-out golden set judged by an independent assurance unit, and is promoted only by a human seat (Standard §2.3 and §2.4).

### 3. Library

The 49GAION Library holds all ready-made AIOC blueprints and part cards, built once, configured per `[ORG]AION`, never rebuilt. Every capability has a fixed test.

### 4. HOAC

HOAC (Human Oversight & Accountability Control) is a label of human accountability, not a unit. It sits on a Cell, a Pod or a whole AIOC, at any depth, with three kinds: `[ORG]HOAC-GOV`, `[ORG]HOAC-TM` or `[ORG]HOAC-ACS`. It holds what only a human can hold: authority, accountability and the stop door. A report, critique, objection or stop reaches the nearest HOAC directly, never through any AI. `[ORG]HOAC-TM` is the human hub: it decides and orders.

### 5. AIOC

An AIOC (AI Operating Core) is an AI unit of one organisation, `[ORG]AIOC-[INITIALS]`: a Panel, a Back office and one versioned blueprint, led by its Chief of Staff. The AIOC is the only unit kind. Its Blueprint holds its Cells (the smallest parts, each a fixed seat with a replaceable human or AI filler) and its Pods (groups of Cells). `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` (Organization Core Office) is the AI hub, under TM: every AIOC, at any depth, joins, is fixed, maintained and upgraded through it; it gives no orders on what work is done. Any unit may be composed of smaller units of either kind, to any depth.

### 6. Protocol

The 49GAION Operating Protocol sets how AIOCs, Pods, Cells and the parts work together: the card, the Work Item, typed ports and the stop door.
