# 49GAION · the central hub

This repository holds the 49GAION Standard, the master definition, the Naming Standard, the unit register form and the decision records.

**The complete definition.** The approved definition of the whole of 49GAION, in one text, is [`49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md`](49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md) (DR-0041). Where any other text differs from it, it governs. 49GAION writes principles and the few safeguards that always hold; the details and the way of working are decided by AI at the time.

## Overview

### 1. What 49GAION is

**49GAION** is an organisation design framework: a framework for building organisations from interchangeable AI and human parts. AION stands for AI Operating Nexus.

**It designs AI-native organisations.**
- Organisations were built around the human, and most now add AI as a tool inside that human design.
- 49GAION starts instead from the final output. It builds the organisation first on what AI can do, from [AIOCs](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION-aioc/blob/main/docs/WHAT-IS-AN-AIOC.md), [Cells and Pods](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION-aioc/blob/main/docs/CELLS-AND-PODS.md), each AIOC led by its [Chief of Staff](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#52-chief-of-staff-and-office).
- It covers the gaps of AI with human seats marked [HOAC](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#41-the-hoac), each with its own Chief of Staff, which hold the authority and the accountability.
- It is live: it gives the design and configuration of the organisation itself to AI, and a human seat decides, so the organisation keeps pace as AI improves, with open access to the 49GAION Library.
- It aims to turn the whole into a successful organisation: one that delivers its output repeatably, at its quality, and without depending on any one person.

The full introduction is in [`49GAION.md`](49GAION.md).

**The product: `[ORG]AION`**, the AI Operating Nexus of one organisation; its one definition is in the Standard, [§1.3, item 1](upstream/49GAION-UPSTREAM.md#13-the-product-orgaion).

**The Human Floor.** Every `[ORG]AION` stands on a Human Floor: the work that always stays human, by law or by its nature (HF-01 to HF-19). Organisations can be more human, never less; the system enforces it. This holds at the handover; after it, each organisation keeps its own floor (DR-0035).

### 2. Self-Improvement

Self-Improvement is a process shared by AI and humans. Any human seat or AI unit may raise a proposal; a proposal carries the current text, the proposed text, the reason, the evidence and the parts and tests it affects; it is discussed, and the discussion decides nothing; it takes effect only on approval by the accountable human seat. No unit approves a change to itself. In an `[ORG]AION` it is held inside `[ORG]HOAC-TM` and carried out with an AIOC within TM, and it runs at every level: each unit improves its own work, and the `[ORG]AION` proposes the changes to the structure, the Standard and the Blueprint (DR-0035); at 49GAION level it is held by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION (Standard §2). Every change is found and tested in a champion-and-challenger loop: a challenger is judged in pairs against the version in force, must win on a held-out golden set judged by an independent assurance unit, and is promoted only by a human seat (Standard §2.3 and §2.4).

**Reading (DR-0041).** Proposing is open to all. Changes to the organisation's own Standard, Blueprint and Library, its rules at the governance level, are applied by the `[ORG]AION` with the governance seat's approval, or within the dial that seat has signed; a proposal that is not right is rejected with its reason, and the proposer may take it to the seat. Improvement inside the operation is Top Management's, under its mandate, and what it builds and learns is sent to the governance level to be recorded.

### 3. Library

The 49GAION Library holds all ready-made AIOC blueprints and part cards, built once, configured per `[ORG]AION`, never rebuilt. Every capability has a fixed test. Its register names every part once, with its card, its fixed test and, for an AIOC part, its mother blueprint held by pin: [`library/README.md`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION-protocol/blob/main/library/README.md) in `49GAION-protocol`. The Library is a marketplace: each `[ORG]AION` takes a part as a starting point, configures it inside the organisation through its own live AI, and may improve it and, by its own choice, give it back (DR-0035, DR-0037).

**Reading (DR-0041).** The Library is the greatest asset of every `[ORG]AION`. Beside blueprints and part cards it holds patterns and models, operating models, instructions and processes, panels and cores, and the patterns of whole organisations. An `[ORG]AION` takes from it and personalises it, and what it has taken is held in its own Library.

### 4. HOAC

HOAC (Human Oversight & Accountability Control) is a label of human accountability, not a unit. It sits on a Cell, a Pod or a whole AIOC, at any depth, with three kinds: `[ORG]HOAC-GOV`, `[ORG]HOAC-TM` or `[ORG]HOAC-ACS`. It holds what only a human can hold: authority, accountability and the stop door. A report, critique, objection or stop reaches the nearest HOAC directly, never through any AI. `[ORG]HOAC-TM` is the human hub: it decides and orders.

### 5. AIOC

An AIOC (AI Operating Core) is an AI unit of one organisation, `[ORG]AIOC-[INITIALS]`: a Panel, a Back office and one versioned blueprint, led by its Chief of Staff. The AIOC is the only unit kind. Its Blueprint holds its Cells (the smallest parts, each a fixed seat with a replaceable human or AI filler) and its Pods (groups of Cells). `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` (Organization Core Office) is the AI hub, under TM: every AIOC, at any depth, joins, is fixed, maintained and upgraded through it; it gives no orders on what work is done. An AIOC may hold other AIOCs, to any depth, and its own Pods and Cells.

**Addition (DR-0041).** The panel is the organisation's own interface, written in code; the back office is the AI chat workspace, an authorised interface that keeps the panel. The hub of the operation, human and AI, is the Top Management Group, the first AIOC under the core; a separate `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`, where an organisation has one, serves the operation under Top Management.

### 6. Protocol

The 49GAION Operating Protocol sets how AIOCs, Pods, Cells and the parts work together: the card, the Work Item, typed ports and the stop door.
